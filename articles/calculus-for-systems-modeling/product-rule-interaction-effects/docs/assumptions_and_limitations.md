# Assumptions and Limitations

## Assumptions

- Factor functions are differentiable or numerically smooth enough for derivative approximation.
- Multiplication is a meaningful model structure for the target system.
- Units are compatible and interpretable.
- Relative or logarithmic growth decompositions are used only for positive nonzero factors.
- Synthetic data is illustrative and not empirical evidence.

## Limitations

- Product-rule decomposition does not establish causality.
- Noisy measurements can distort derivative estimates.
- Near-zero factors can destabilize proportional decompositions.
- Aggregates can hide distributional consequences.
- Multiplicative model structure may oversimplify institutional or ecological reality.
