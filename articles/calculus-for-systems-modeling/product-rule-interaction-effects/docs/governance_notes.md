# Governance Notes

Product-rule decompositions often appear in domains where attribution matters: emissions, risk, revenue, exposure, vulnerability, performance, and institutional outcomes.

Before using outputs in public reasoning, ask:

1. Are the factors meaningfully multiplied?
2. Are the factors measured reliably?
3. Are the derivatives numerically stable?
4. Is the decomposition descriptive or causal?
5. Does the aggregate hide unequal burdens?
6. What assumptions should be visible to readers?
7. What decisions could be influenced by this decomposition?
