# Article Notes: The Product Rule and Interaction Effects

The product rule is not only a differentiation rule. In systems modeling, it is a practical tool for interpreting interacting factors.

## Product Rule

\[
\frac{d}{dt}(a(t)b(t)) = a'(t)b(t) + a(t)b'(t)
\]

## Interpretation

- \(a'(t)b(t)\): change in \(a\), weighted by the current level of \(b\)
- \(a(t)b'(t)\): change in \(b\), weighted by the current level of \(a\)

## Applications

- Revenue: price × quantity
- Risk: probability × consequence
- Environmental impact: population × affluence × technology
- Exposure harm: exposure × vulnerability
- Stock-flow model: rate coefficient × stock
- Optimization objectives with multiplicative terms
