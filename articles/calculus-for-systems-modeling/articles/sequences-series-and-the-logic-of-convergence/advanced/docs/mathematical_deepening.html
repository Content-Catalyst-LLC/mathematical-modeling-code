Mathematical Deepening

Sequences and series are limit structures. In systems modeling, convergence reasoning separates finite computation from stable limiting interpretation and prevents repeated calculation from being mistaken for mathematical reliability.

Formal Convergence Conditions

Sequence Definition

A convergence claim must define the ordered terms, states, estimates, or errors.

Partial Sum

An infinite series is defined by the limiting behavior of partial sums.

Stopping Rule

The reason computation ended must be documented separately from convergence.

Remainder Bound

Finite approximations require tail or error logic before they support limit claims.
