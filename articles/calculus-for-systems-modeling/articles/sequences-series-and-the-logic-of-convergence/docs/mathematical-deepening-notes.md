# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- sequence versus series;
- term sequence versus partial-sum sequence;
- convergence of terms versus convergence of accumulated totals;
- finite partial sum versus infinite series;
- stopped computation versus convergence;
- latest-term smallness versus tail error;
- absolute convergence versus conditional convergence;
- net cancellation versus gross accumulated activity;
- mathematical limit versus modeling validity.

## Pathology checklist

- terms approach zero but the series diverges;
- finite computation is presented as an infinite-total result;
- stopping rule is undocumented;
- no remainder bound or tail estimate is reported;
- conditional convergence hides large offsetting activity;
- stable-looking early partial sums hide slow divergence;
- convergence under one tolerance fails under another;
- geometric feedback ratio is outside the convergence range.
