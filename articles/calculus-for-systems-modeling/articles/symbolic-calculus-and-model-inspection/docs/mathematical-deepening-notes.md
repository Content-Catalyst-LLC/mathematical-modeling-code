# Mathematical Deepening Notes

Symbolic model inspection connects expression trees, algebraic simplification, differentiation rules, integration methods, limits, series expansion, Jacobian linearization, equilibrium solving, Hessian curvature, assumptions, domains, and exact-to-numeric workflow design.

## Required distinctions

- original expression versus transformed expression
- symbolic result versus empirical validity
- exact expression versus valid domain
- simplification versus assumption loss
- derivative sign versus parameter regime
- equilibrium candidate versus stable equilibrium
- Jacobian linearization versus nonlinear dynamics
- symbolic benchmark versus real-world validation

## Review checklist

- Preserve original expression before transformation.
- Record variables, parameters, units, domains, and excluded values.
- Record derivative, integral, limit, equilibrium, Jacobian, and Hessian results.
- Preserve assumptions and parameter regimes.
- Compare symbolic outputs with numerical behavior when useful.
- Treat simplification as a reviewed transformation.
