Mathematical Deepening

This advanced layer treats differentiation rules as structural preservation results for derivative operators over addition, multiplication, division, composition, inversion, constraints, and logarithmic transformation.

Formal Structural Rules

Linearity

The derivative operator satisfies \(D(f+g)=Df+Dg\) and \(D(cf)=cDf\) on differentiable functions.

Leibniz Rule

The product rule \(D(fg)=fDg+gDf\) makes the derivative a derivation over multiplication.

Chain Rule

The chain rule says local linear maps compose. In multivariable models, this is matrix multiplication.

Implicit Rule

Implicit differentiation requires regularity conditions, such as a nonzero partial derivative with respect to the dependent variable.
