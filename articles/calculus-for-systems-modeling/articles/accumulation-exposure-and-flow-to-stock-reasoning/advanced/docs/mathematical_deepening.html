Mathematical Deepening

Flow-to-stock reasoning is an application of integral accumulation to state variables. In systems modeling, it creates an audit trail from rates to cumulative stocks, exposures, burdens, and lagged consequences.

Formal Flow-to-Stock Conditions

Initial Condition

The inherited stock before interval accumulation must be stated.

Net Flow

Stock change comes from inflow minus outflow under a stated sign convention.

Exposure Window

Exposure claims depend on intensity, duration, pathway, population, and window.

Unit Audit

Flow multiplied by time must produce the stated stock or exposure units.
