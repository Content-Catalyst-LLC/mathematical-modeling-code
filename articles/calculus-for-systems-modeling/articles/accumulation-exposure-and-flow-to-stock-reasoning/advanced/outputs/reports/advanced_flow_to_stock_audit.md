# Advanced Mathematical Audit: Accumulation, Exposure, and Flow-to-Stock Reasoning

## Formal topics included

- Flow-to-stock equations
- Initial conditions
- Net flow and gross flows
- Cumulative exposure
- Population-weighted exposure
- Unit consistency
- Measurement windows
- Accumulated exposure versus modeled consequence

## Modeling implication

A responsible accumulation workflow should state the stock, flows, initial condition, sign convention, units, measurement window, data resolution, numerical method, and distinction between net change, gross activity, exposure, and consequence.
