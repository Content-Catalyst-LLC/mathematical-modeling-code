# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- flow versus stock;
- inflow, outflow, and net flow;
- cumulative inflow versus cumulative outflow;
- net stock change versus gross activity;
- exposure versus concentration;
- accumulated exposure versus modeled harm;
- initial condition versus interval accumulation;
- measurement window versus lifetime accumulation;
- continuous integral versus discrete approximation.

## Pathology checklist

- rate reduction misread as stock reduction;
- peak exposure misread as cumulative exposure;
- net change hides large offsetting gross flows;
- inherited stock omitted from ending-stock interpretation;
- exposure window unstated;
- units do not reconcile between flow and stock;
- missing data silently treated as zero;
- population or vulnerability weights used without explanation.
