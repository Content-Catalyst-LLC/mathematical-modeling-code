# Mechanistic Explanation and the Limits of Formalism Notes

Mechanistic explanation and the limits of formalism connect differential equations, causal structure, state variables, model semantics, parameter interpretation, identifiability, sensitivity analysis, validation, counterfactual reasoning, model comparison, abstraction, idealization, and computational governance.

## Required distinctions

- formal dependence versus causal dependence
- equation fit versus mechanism evidence
- calibrated parameter versus causal parameter
- formal consistency versus explanatory validity
- predictive model versus mechanistic model
- exploratory model versus confirmed mechanism
- abstraction versus omitted critical structure

## Review checklist

- Preserve mechanism records, entities, activities, relations, and evidence status.
- Document variables, parameters, units, sources, ranges, and model roles.
- Separate descriptive, predictive, exploratory, mechanistic, and decision-support claims.
- Attach validation scope and claim boundaries.
- Flag black-box transformations and unsupported causal claims.
- Export explanation governance records as CSV, JSON, and Markdown.
