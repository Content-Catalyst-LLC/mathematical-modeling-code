# Mechanism and Formalism Audit

## Mechanism Records
- **stock_flow_accumulation**: stock changes through inflow and outflow. Evidence: synthetic teaching example. A stock-flow equation is mechanistic only when flows represent real processes.
- **balancing_feedback**: state-dependent adjustment limits growth or change. Evidence: formal teaching example. Feedback parameters require process interpretation and evidence.
- **threshold_transition**: behavior changes after a critical value is crossed. Evidence: scenario-based example. Threshold claims require careful scope and uncertainty notes.

## Formal Representation Records
- **differential_equation** (dx/dt = f(x,t,theta)): describes state change over time. Requirement: identify what process f represents. A rate equation without process interpretation may be descriptive only.
- **parameter** (theta): controls model behavior. Requirement: record unit, source, range, and mechanism meaning. Calibrated parameters are not automatically causal quantities.
- **constraint** (x <= K): bounds system behavior. Requirement: explain whether K is physical, institutional, ecological, or assumed. Constraints can hide strong assumptions.

## Explanation Claim Records
- **mechanistic**: explains how an organized process can produce behavior. Scope: applies only where the mechanism and assumptions hold. Status: review.
- **predictive**: forecasts output under specified conditions. Scope: limited to validated domain and time horizon. Status: review.
- **exploratory**: investigates possible system behavior. Scope: not a confirmed mechanism or forecast. Status: active.

Formal structure supports explanation only when mechanism, evidence, and scope are documented.
