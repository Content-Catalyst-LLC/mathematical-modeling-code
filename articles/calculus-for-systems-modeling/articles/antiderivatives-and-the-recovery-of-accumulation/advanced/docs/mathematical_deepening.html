Mathematical Deepening

Antiderivatives recover families of possible accumulated quantities from rate functions. Their modeling interpretation depends on rate definition, interval, initial condition, domain, units, and whether recovery is symbolic or numerical.

Formal Recovery Conditions

Antiderivative Family

If F' = f, then F + C is also an antiderivative. A rate alone does not determine baseline.

Initial Condition

A starting value selects one recovered trajectory from the family.

Flow-to-Stock

A stock equals its initial value plus accumulated net flow.

Numerical Accumulation

Discrete recovery depends on method, time step, data quality, and unit consistency.
