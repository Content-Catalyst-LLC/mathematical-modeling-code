# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- derivative versus antiderivative;
- rate versus recovered quantity;
- symbolic antiderivative versus numerical accumulation;
- indefinite integral versus definite accumulated change;
- constant of integration versus initial condition;
- stock versus flow;
- marginal quantity versus total quantity;
- recovery from model rates versus recovery from observed rate data.

## Pathology checklist

- missing initial condition;
- missing inflow or outflow;
- wrong integration variable;
- unit mismatch;
- irregular or coarse sampling;
- noise accumulated over time;
- extrapolated rate outside domain;
- treating an indefinite integral as a unique recovered state.
