# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- integration by parts as calculation versus decomposition;
- product rule origin versus symbolic formula;
- direct integral versus boundary-minus-residual expression;
- boundary term versus residual integral;
- choice of parts as interpretive decision;
- accounting identity versus causal explanation;
- analytic equality versus numerical residual;
- meaningful decomposition versus algebraic clutter.

## Pathology checklist

- units do not match across terms;
- boundary term interpreted without interval context;
- residual term depends on unstable numerical derivatives;
- decomposition identity misrepresented as causal attribution;
- choice of parts produces terms with no modeling meaning;
- direct and decomposed numerical estimates disagree beyond tolerance;
- repeated decomposition creates complexity without insight.
