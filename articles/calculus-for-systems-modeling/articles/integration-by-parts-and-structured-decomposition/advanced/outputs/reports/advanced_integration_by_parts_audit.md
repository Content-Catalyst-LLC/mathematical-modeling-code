# Advanced Mathematical Audit: Integration by Parts and Structured Decomposition

## Formal topics included

- Product-rule origin
- Boundary terms
- Residual integrals
- Choice of parts
- Weighted accumulation
- Unit consistency
- Direct-versus-decomposed numerical residuals
- Causal overclaim review

## Modeling implication

A useful integration-by-parts decomposition should report direct accumulation, boundary contribution, residual accumulation, decomposition residual, units, interval, numerical method, and interpretive purpose.
