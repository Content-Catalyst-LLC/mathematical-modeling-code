# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- divergence theorem versus Stokes' theorem
- boundary flux versus boundary circulation
- closed surface versus open surface
- enclosed volume versus unrelated interior grid
- outward normal versus inward normal
- surface mesh approximation versus volume-cell approximation
- local divergence versus total source-sink balance
- physical conservation versus abstract state-space expansion
- theorem equality versus numerical conservation audit
- field definition versus theorem calculation

## Review checklist

- Define the vector field and its units.
- State the closed surface and enclosed volume.
- Confirm the surface fully encloses the volume.
- Document outward-normal direction on all surface patches.
- Explain what divergence means in the modeled system.
- Report surface mesh resolution, volume grid resolution, and divergence-computation method.
- Compare boundary flux and volume divergence under refinement when possible.
