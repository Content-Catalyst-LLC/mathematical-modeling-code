# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- structural relationship versus rate relationship;
- variable value versus derivative;
- driving rate versus inferred target rate;
- structural derivative versus observed motion;
- explicit rate propagation versus implicit rate coupling;
- tangent velocity versus unconstrained velocity;
- local operating point versus global relationship;
- measurement noise versus structural uncertainty.

## Pathology checklist

- hidden variables omitted;
- relationship changes over time;
- units are inconsistent;
- operating point not stated;
- derivative or Jacobian is near singular;
- noisy finite differences amplify error;
- related rate interpreted beyond local regime.
