Mathematical Deepening

Related rates are derivatives along a trajectory. If \(y=f(x(t))\), then \(dy/dt=Df(x(t))x'(t)\). If \(F(x(t))=0\), then allowed velocities satisfy \(DF(x(t))x'(t)=0\), so rate relationships are tangent-space relationships.

Formal Related-Rates Conditions

Trajectory

Variables are time-parametrized paths through a state space.

Relationship

A function or constraint converts one rate into another.

Operating Point

The derivative is evaluated at the current state and may vary across regimes.

Conditioning

Small or singular rate-conversion derivatives can destabilize inference.
