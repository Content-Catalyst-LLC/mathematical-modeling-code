# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- derivative versus elasticity;
- marginal response versus average response;
- absolute sensitivity versus normalized sensitivity;
- local sensitivity versus global sensitivity;
- scale-dependent quantity versus dimensionless quantity;
- analytic derivative versus finite-difference estimate;
- model sensitivity versus causal effect;
- proportional response versus absolute response.

## Pathology checklist

- elasticity near zero input;
- elasticity near zero output;
- negative or sign-changing domains;
- unstable finite-difference step size;
- local sensitivity generalized globally;
- sensitivity reported without baseline;
- parameter range omitted;
- threshold or discontinuity ignored.
