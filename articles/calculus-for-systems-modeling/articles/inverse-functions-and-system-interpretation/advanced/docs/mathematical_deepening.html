Mathematical Deepening

Inverse interpretation depends on injectivity, domain choice, local invertibility, and conditioning. The inverse is defined on the image of the chosen domain, not automatically on the entire codomain. Local inverse claims require nonzero derivative or invertible Jacobian conditions.

Formal Inverse-Function Conditions

Injectivity

Different admissible inputs must produce different outputs for unique inversion.

Image

The inverse is defined on outputs actually generated by the admissible domain.

Local Invertibility

A nonzero derivative or invertible Jacobian supports local inverse recovery.

Conditioning

Near-singular forward maps make inverse recovery unstable under noise.
