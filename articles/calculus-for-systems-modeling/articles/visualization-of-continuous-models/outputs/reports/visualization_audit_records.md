# Visualization Audit Records

- **logistic_growth_scenario_trajectories** (trajectory_plot): The figure shows model-implied trajectories under selected assumptions, not empirical forecasts.
- **phase_portrait_review** (phase_portrait): Phase portraits show local and geometric behavior, not automatic empirical validity.
- **vector_field_review** (vector_field): Vector fields require unit and boundary interpretation.
- **uncertainty_band_review** (uncertainty_band): Uncertainty visualization can imply probability without support if not defined.
- **diagnostic_plot_review** (diagnostic_plot): A clean primary graph may hide numerical artifacts without diagnostics.
