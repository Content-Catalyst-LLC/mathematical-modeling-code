# Mathematical Deepening Notes

Visualization of continuous models connects topology, geometry, differential equations, vector calculus, numerical analysis, uncertainty quantification, interpolation, dimensionality reduction, response surfaces, solver diagnostics, and visual encoding.

## Required distinctions

- trajectory versus phase portrait
- solver output versus empirical observation
- scenario spread versus probability interval
- visual smoothness versus numerical reliability
- output sampling versus solver step history
- field direction versus magnitude scaling
- surface interpolation versus known values
- clear communication versus unsupported confidence

## Review checklist

- Preserve source trajectory tables behind figures.
- Document parameters, units, initial conditions, and solver settings.
- Record axes, scale transformations, figure type, and visible domain.
- Record color scale, grid resolution, smoothing, and interpolation.
- Preserve uncertainty definitions and scenario selection rationale.
- Use diagnostics for error, residuals, solver steps, and constraints.
