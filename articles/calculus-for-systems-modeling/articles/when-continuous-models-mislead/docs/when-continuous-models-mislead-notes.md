# When Continuous Models Mislead Notes

Continuous model misuse connects local approximation, differentiability assumptions, smoothness, structural breaks, discontinuities, singularities, bifurcations, stiffness, discretization, sensitivity, identifiability, model misspecification, numerical stability, validation, and governance.

## Required distinctions

- continuous approximation versus real-world continuity
- smooth curve versus structural break
- equilibrium existence versus path safety
- aggregate average versus distribution
- numerical solution versus validated explanation
- curve fit versus mechanism
- local model domain versus extrapolated domain

## Review checklist

- Document smoothness assumptions for variables, rates, and fields.
- Check thresholds, discontinuities, and structural breaks.
- Record parameter ranges, sensitivity checks, and stress tests.
- Preserve solver methods, step size, tolerance, convergence status, and warnings.
- Identify omitted mechanisms and aggregation risks.
- Attach validation scope and claim boundaries.
