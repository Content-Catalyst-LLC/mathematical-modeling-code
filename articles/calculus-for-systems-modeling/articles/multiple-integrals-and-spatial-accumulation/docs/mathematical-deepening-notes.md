# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- point value versus spatial total
- density versus total quantity
- area density versus volume density
- region boundary versus coordinate range
- analytic integral versus numerical grid approximation
- rectangular domain versus irregular masked domain
- area-weighted average versus population-weighted average
- coordinate transformation versus unchanged measure
- spatial total versus distributional pattern

## Review checklist

- State the integrand and its units.
- Define the region or volume.
- State the area or volume element.
- Document coordinate system and projection assumptions.
- Report grid resolution when numerical.
- Report cell area and mask logic.
- Compare totals and averages where useful.
- Preserve weighted and distributional diagnostics.
- Document boundary and aggregation risk.
