# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- linear rate law versus nonlinear rate law
- proportional response versus changing response
- power term versus interaction term
- saturation versus hard threshold
- equilibrium existence versus equilibrium stability
- local linearization versus global behavior
- phase behavior versus a single trajectory
- solver artifact versus modeled nonlinear behavior
- threshold evidence versus threshold assumption
- mathematical complexity versus system validity

## Review checklist

- Define the state variable and meaningful domain.
- Explain every nonlinear term.
- Document parameters, units, sources, and uncertainty.
- Identify equilibria and stability where appropriate.
- Document thresholds and carrying capacity assumptions.
- Record solver method, time horizon, step size, and tolerances.
- Test sensitivity to initial conditions, parameters, and solver choices.
- Explain whether outputs are theoretical, descriptive, exploratory, scenario-based, or predictive.
