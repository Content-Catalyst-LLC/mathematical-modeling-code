# Resource Depletion and Regeneration Notes

Resource models connect stocks, extraction, renewal, loss, carrying capacity, thresholds, sustainable yield, governance, uncertainty, and claim boundaries.

## Required distinctions

- renewable versus unlimited
- stock level versus rate of change
- extraction flow versus cumulative extraction
- regeneration assumption versus observed recovery
- maximum sustainable yield versus precautionary harvest
- nonrenewable drawdown versus renewable recovery
- efficiency improvement versus reduced total extraction
- model scenario versus management prediction

## Review checklist

- Define the resource stock, unit, quality dimension, boundary, and measurement status.
- Document extraction records, hidden flows, leakage, waste, and enforcement limits.
- Document regeneration function, carrying capacity, threshold values, and environmental drivers.
- Separate renewable-resource scenarios from nonrenewable drawdown.
- Attach uncertainty, sensitivity, governance assumptions, validation scope, and claim boundaries.
