# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- diffusion versus transport
- transport versus wave propagation
- field value versus flux
- gradient-driven flux versus velocity-driven flux
- source term versus boundary inflow
- sink term versus boundary outflow
- boundary condition versus initial condition
- conservation equation versus empirical trend surface
- numerical instability versus modeled instability
- computed spatial field versus measured spatial data

## Review checklist

- Define the state field, units, and domain.
- Document diffusion coefficient and transport velocity.
- Record source and sink terms.
- Explain boundary behavior and domain extent.
- Save diffusion and transport ratios in audit outputs.
- Check grid spacing, time step, and stability.
- Compare boundary assumptions where conclusions depend on them.
- Distinguish model-generated spatial fields from observations.
