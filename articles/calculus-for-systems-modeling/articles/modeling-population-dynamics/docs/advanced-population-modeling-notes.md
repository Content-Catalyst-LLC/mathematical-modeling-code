# Advanced Population Dynamics Notes

This article folder includes advanced population modeling scaffolds for exponential growth, logistic growth, Allee thresholds, harvesting/removal, stochastic logistic paths, two-patch migration, Leslie/stage-structured populations, spatial diffusion-style updates, calibration grid search, and identifiability diagnostics.

## Governance warnings

- A growth law is an assumption, not a universal description.
- Carrying capacity is assumption-bearing and may change over time.
- Threshold parameters can be difficult to identify from limited data.
- A fitted curve does not automatically prove the model mechanism.
- Stochastic output should be interpreted as a distribution, not a single path.
