# Case Study: Modeling Population Dynamics Notes

Population dynamics connects differential equations, equilibria, stability, nonlinear feedback, sensitivity analysis, calibration, identifiability, uncertainty quantification, model comparison, stochastic effects, spatial structure, age structure, and governance.

## Required distinctions

- state variable versus measured population
- exponential baseline versus long-run forecast
- logistic growth versus universal population law
- carrying capacity parameter versus fixed fact
- calibration fit versus mechanism evidence
- synthetic teaching data versus empirical evidence
- scenario output versus prediction

## Review checklist

- Define the counted population, unit, boundary, and time scale.
- Document initial population, growth rate, carrying capacity, units, sources, and ranges.
- Compare exponential and logistic assumptions where useful.
- Record calibration status, sensitivity, uncertainty, and validation scope.
- Attach claim boundaries to all outputs.
