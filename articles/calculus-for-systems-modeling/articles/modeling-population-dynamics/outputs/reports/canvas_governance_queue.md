# Population Dynamics Canvas Queue

- **Growth Law**: A growth law is an assumption, not a universal description.
- **Threshold Dynamics**: Threshold parameters can be difficult to identify from limited data.
- **Stochastic Structure**: A single stochastic path is not a distribution.
- **Structured Population**: Aggregate population size may hide reproductive composition.
- **Identifiability**: A fitted curve does not automatically prove parameter meaning or mechanism.
