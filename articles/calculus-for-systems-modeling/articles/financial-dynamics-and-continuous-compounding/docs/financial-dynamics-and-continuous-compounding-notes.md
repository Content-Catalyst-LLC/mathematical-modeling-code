# Financial Dynamics and Continuous Compounding Notes

Financial dynamics connect rates, value, time, cash flows, compounding, discounting, debt, inflation, returns, volatility, liquidity, leverage, and claim boundaries.

## Required distinctions

- nominal versus real rates
- effective versus stated rates
- discrete versus continuous compounding
- expected return versus guaranteed outcome
- arithmetic average versus geometric return
- future value versus present value
- asset growth versus debt growth
- cash-flow calculation versus financial advice
- scenario versus prediction

## Review checklist

- Define value type, rate convention, time unit, and compounding frequency.
- Preserve cash-flow timing, sign, uncertainty, and inflation basis.
- Document discount-rate rationale and sensitivity.
- Match debt formulas to contract terms.
- Include volatility, drawdown, liquidity, fees, taxes, and risk where relevant.
- Attach uncertainty, sensitivity, governance context, validation scope, and claim boundaries.
