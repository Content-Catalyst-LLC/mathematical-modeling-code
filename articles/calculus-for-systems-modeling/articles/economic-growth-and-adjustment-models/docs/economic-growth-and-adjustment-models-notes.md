# Economic Growth and Adjustment Models Notes

Economic growth models connect output, rates, accumulation, investment, depreciation, productivity, labor, adjustment, shocks, constraints, distribution, welfare, uncertainty, and claim boundaries.

## Required distinctions

- output growth versus welfare
- real versus nominal output
- levels versus rates
- stock versus flow
- productivity mechanism versus residual
- capital accumulation versus capital quality
- unconstrained growth versus constrained growth
- scenario versus forecast
- aggregate growth versus distribution

## Review checklist

- Define output measure, price basis, sector boundary, and data vintage.
- Document growth-rate assumptions and compounding horizon.
- Preserve capital, investment, depreciation, and productivity records.
- Document labor assumptions, adjustment speeds, delays, and shock terms.
- Compare unconstrained and constrained scenarios.
- Attach distributional notes, uncertainty, sensitivity, validation scope, and claim boundaries.
