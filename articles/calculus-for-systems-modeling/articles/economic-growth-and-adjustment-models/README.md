# Economic Growth and Adjustment Models

Advanced companion code and reproducible workflows for **Economic Growth and Adjustment Models** in the **Calculus for Systems Modeling** series.

## Themes

- economic output and growth rates
- exponential growth and compounding
- constrained growth and saturation
- capital accumulation, investment, and depreciation
- production functions and productivity assumptions
- labor, population, participation, and skill
- adjustment toward equilibrium
- shocks, delays, overshoot, and recovery
- infrastructure, resource, financial, and institutional constraints
- distribution, welfare, and quality of growth
- SQL governance registries
- Haskell typed economic records
- Catalyst Canvas governance
- reusable calculators

## Run

```bash
make smoke
make all
make calculators
make advanced
```
