# Advanced Mathematical Audit: Implicit Differentiation and Coupled Relationships

## Formal topics included

- Implicit relations and local branches
- Total differentials and tangent directions
- Implicit function theorem
- Regularity conditions
- Equilibrium sensitivity
- Jacobian conditioning
- Singular cases and loss of local solvability

## Modeling implication

An implicit derivative should state the constraint, branch, regularity condition, conditioning status, and the interpretation of the derivative as local co-adjustment under a coupled relationship.
