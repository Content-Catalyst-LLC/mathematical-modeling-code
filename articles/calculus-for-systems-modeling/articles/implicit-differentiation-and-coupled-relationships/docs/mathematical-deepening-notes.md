# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- implicit relation versus explicit function;
- local branch versus global solvability;
- total differential versus ordinary derivative;
- tangent space versus unconstrained movement;
- regular point versus singular point;
- scalar denominator condition versus vector Jacobian invertibility;
- co-adjustment rate versus causal response;
- numerical conditioning versus formal invertibility.

## Pathology checklist

- denominator or Jacobian near zero;
- vertical tangent;
- multiple branches;
- singular equilibrium;
- ill-conditioned linear system;
- finite-difference mismatch;
- local derivative extended across a regime change.
