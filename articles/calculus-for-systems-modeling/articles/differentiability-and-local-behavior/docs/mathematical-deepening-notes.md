# Mathematical Deepening Notes

This article should be advanced enough for mathematicians. It includes real-analysis, functional-analysis, nonsmooth-analysis, and numerical-diagnostic ideas beyond an introductory derivative explainer.

## Required distinctions

- derivative as a difference-quotient limit;
- differentiability as local linear approximation;
- continuity versus differentiability;
- one-sided derivative versus two-sided derivative;
- partial derivatives versus full differentiability;
- directional derivatives versus Fréchet derivative;
- Gâteaux differentiability versus Fréchet differentiability;
- gradient/Jacobian as local map;
- symbolic derivative versus numerical derivative;
- differentiability in the formula versus validity in the modeling domain.

## Pathology checklist

- continuous but not differentiable functions;
- all partial derivatives exist but differentiability fails;
- derivative exists but is discontinuous;
- finite-difference false smoothness;
- kink hidden between sample points;
- boundary derivatives on constrained domains;
- nonsmooth max/min/absolute-value models;
- thresholds and active-constraint changes.
