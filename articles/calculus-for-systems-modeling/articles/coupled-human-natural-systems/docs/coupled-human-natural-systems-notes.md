# Coupled Human-Natural Systems Notes

Coupled human-natural systems models connect population, demand, livelihoods, institutions, infrastructure, resource stocks, regeneration, ecosystems, climate stress, thresholds, adaptation, governance, resilience, vulnerability, and distributional burden.

## Required distinctions

- human systems versus homogeneous pressure terms
- natural systems versus passive resource stocks
- extraction versus regeneration
- ecological feedback versus governance feedback
- local improvement versus externalized impacts
- adaptation capacity versus automatic adaptation
- aggregate efficiency versus justice and resilience
- scenario analysis versus prediction

## Review checklist

- Define the coupled boundary, spatial scale, time scale, state variables, and external flows.
- Document human assumptions, natural assumptions, coupling mechanisms, extraction functions, regeneration functions, governance response, thresholds, adaptation, and distributional effects.
- Preserve uncertainty ranges, sensitivity results, data sources, validation scope, omitted mechanisms, and claim boundaries.
