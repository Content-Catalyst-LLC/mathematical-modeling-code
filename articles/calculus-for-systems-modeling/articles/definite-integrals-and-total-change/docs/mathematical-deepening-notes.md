# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- definite integral versus antiderivative;
- signed accumulation versus absolute accumulation;
- net change versus total activity;
- interval bounds versus indefinite recovery;
- geometric area versus modeled accumulated quantity;
- continuous ideal integral versus numerical approximation;
- integrand definition versus cumulative interpretation;
- rate units versus accumulated units.

## Pathology checklist

- wrong interval bounds;
- wrong integration variable;
- missing sign convention;
- signed cancellation misread as no activity;
- unit mismatch;
- coarse grid;
- missed peaks or discontinuities;
- total from sampled data reported without numerical method.
