Mathematical Deepening

Definite integrals are interval-based accumulation functionals. Their modeling interpretation depends on integrand definition, interval bounds, orientation, sign convention, units, and whether the integral is analytic or numerical.

Formal Total-Change Conditions

Integrand

The integrand must represent a rate, density, intensity, marginal quantity, or distributed measure.

Interval

Total change is defined over explicit lower and upper bounds.

Sign Convention

Signed integrals estimate net change; absolute-value integrals estimate total activity.

Numerical Method

Approximation quality depends on grid, method, interpolation, smoothness, and missing data.
