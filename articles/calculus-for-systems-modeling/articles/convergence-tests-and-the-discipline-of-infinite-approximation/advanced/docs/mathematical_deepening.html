Mathematical Deepening

Convergence tests are audit methods for infinite approximation. They connect finite partial calculations to limiting claims only when the selected test, its conditions, and its remainder logic are explicitly documented.

Formal Convergence-Test Conditions

Test Selection

The selected test should match the algebraic and asymptotic structure of the series.

Test Conditions

Each test has assumptions that must be checked before the conclusion is valid.

Remainder Estimate

Finite approximations need error or tail logic before supporting infinite-total claims.

Limited Claims

Inconclusive tests require another test or a narrower statement.
