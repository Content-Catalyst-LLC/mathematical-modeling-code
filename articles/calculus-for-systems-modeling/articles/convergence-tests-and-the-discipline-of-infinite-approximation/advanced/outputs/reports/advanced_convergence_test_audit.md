# Advanced Mathematical Audit: Convergence Tests and Infinite Approximation

## Formal topics included

- Term test
- Geometric and p-series tests
- Comparison and limit comparison
- Ratio and root tests
- Integral test tail bounds
- Alternating-series error estimates
- Absolute and conditional convergence
- Remainders and stopping rules

## Modeling implication

A responsible convergence-test workflow should identify the selected test, verify its conditions, report the finite partial sum, state the stopping rule, estimate the remainder where possible, and limit the claim when evidence is inconclusive.
