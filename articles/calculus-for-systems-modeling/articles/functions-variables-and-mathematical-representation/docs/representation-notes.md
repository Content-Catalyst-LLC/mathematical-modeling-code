# Representation Notes

Functions, variables, and mathematical representation determine what a calculus-based systems model can express.

## Review questions

- What quantity does each variable represent?
- What units and scale are used?
- Which variables are states, flows, inputs, outputs, decisions, or parameters?
- What functional form is being assumed?
- What system boundary is being drawn?
- Which assumptions make the representation possible?
- What uncertainty or omitted structure remains?

## Principle

A precise equation cannot repair a poorly defined representation.
