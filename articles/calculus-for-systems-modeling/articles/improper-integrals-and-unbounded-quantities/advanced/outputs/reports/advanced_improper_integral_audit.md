# Advanced Mathematical Audit: Improper Integrals and Unbounded Quantities

## Formal topics included

- Infinite intervals
- Singular endpoints

A responsible improper-integral workflow should state the limiting process, convergence evidence, truncation cutoffs, tail estimates, unit meaning, numerical sensitivity, and model-validity boundary.
