Mathematical Deepening

Improper integrals are ordinary integrals interpreted through limiting processes. In systems modeling, they create a disciplined audit for infinite horizons, long tails, singular endpoints, convergence evidence, truncation sensitivity, and boundary validity.

Formal Improper-Integral Conditions

Limit Definition

An improper integral must state how the problematic boundary is approached.

Convergence

A finite limiting value is required before a total can be reported as finite.

Tail Review

Long-run accumulation depends on tail decay, not just whether the rate approaches zero.

Truncation

Finite cutoffs should be reported separately from infinite-horizon claims.
