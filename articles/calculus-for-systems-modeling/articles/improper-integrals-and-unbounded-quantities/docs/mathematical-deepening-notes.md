# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- ordinary definite integrals versus improper integrals;
- infinite intervals versus singular finite intervals;
- convergence versus divergence;
- finite numerical cutoff versus infinite-horizon limit;
- tail behavior versus early behavior;
- signed convergence versus absolute convergence;
- model validity boundary versus mathematical limit;
- truncation estimate versus convergence proof.

## Pathology checklist

- rate approaches zero but tail still diverges;
- unbounded endpoint incorrectly treated as infinite accumulation without checking convergence;
- finite cutoff presented as infinite-horizon result;
- numerical stability mistaken for proof;
- tail contribution omitted from decision interpretation;
- model extended beyond its credible domain near a singularity;
- signed cancellation hides divergent absolute accumulation.
