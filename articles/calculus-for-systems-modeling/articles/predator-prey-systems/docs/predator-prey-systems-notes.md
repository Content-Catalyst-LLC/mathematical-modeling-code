# Predator-Prey Systems Notes

Predator-prey systems connect coupled differential equations, nonlinear interaction terms, phase-plane analysis, nullclines, equilibrium, Jacobians, eigenvalues, local stability, functional responses, harvesting, stochasticity, calibration, identifiability, uncertainty, and model governance.

## Required distinctions

- modeled cycle versus confirmed ecological mechanism
- mass-action encounter term versus observed predation process
- classic Lotka-Volterra model versus applied predator-prey model
- Type I, Type II, and Type III functional responses
- curve fit versus mechanism evidence
- deterministic trajectory versus stochastic risk distribution
- teaching scenario versus empirical forecast or management recommendation

## Review checklist

- Define prey and predator variables, units, boundaries, and measurement sources.
- Document interaction terms, functional response, and encounter assumptions.
- Preserve parameter records for alpha, beta, gamma, delta, K, attack rate, handling time, stochasticity, and harvesting.
- Record nullclines, equilibria, Jacobian, and stability status.
- Compare functional responses and management terms when claims require them.
- Attach uncertainty, sensitivity, identifiability diagnostics, and claim boundaries.
