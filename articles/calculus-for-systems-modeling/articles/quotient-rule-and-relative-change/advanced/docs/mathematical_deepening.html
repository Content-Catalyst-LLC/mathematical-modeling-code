Mathematical Deepening

The quotient rule follows from writing \(f/g=f\cdot g^{-1}\), applying the product rule, and differentiating the reciprocal. Its interpretation depends on nonzero denominators, positivity for relative-rate claims, and meaningful normalization.

Formal Quotient-Rule Conditions

Nonzero Denominator

The quotient rule is valid on the domain where \(g(x)\neq 0\).

Relative Rate

For positive \(f\) and \(g\), \(R'/R=f'/f-g'/g\).

Ratio Stability

A positive ratio is locally stable when numerator and denominator relative rates match.

Denominator Amplification

The \(g(x)^2\) term can amplify derivative magnitude near small denominators.
