# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- quotient as product with reciprocal;
- derivative of reciprocal and nonzero-denominator condition;
- numerator effect versus denominator effect;
- absolute derivative versus relative derivative;
- logarithmic derivative of a ratio;
- elasticity of a ratio;
- ratio indicator versus causal mechanism;
- formal differentiability versus meaningful normalization.

## Pathology checklist

- denominator equals zero;
- denominator near zero;
- numerator improves while ratio worsens;
- stable ratio with changing numerator and denominator;
- invalid denominator as scaling variable;
- noisy denominator dominates derivative estimate;
- logarithmic derivative used without positivity.
