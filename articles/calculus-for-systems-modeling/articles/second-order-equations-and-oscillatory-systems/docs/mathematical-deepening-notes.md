# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- first derivative versus second derivative
- position state versus velocity state
- homogeneous equation versus forced equation
- restoring force versus damping force
- natural frequency versus forcing frequency
- undamped, underdamped, critically damped, and overdamped behavior
- resonance as structural amplification rather than general cyclicity
- phase-space representation versus scalar trajectory
- solver-generated oscillation versus modeled oscillation
- linear oscillator assumption versus real system evidence

## Review checklist

- Define the state variable and first derivative.
- Explain the meaning of acceleration in context.
- Document damping, restoring, and forcing assumptions.
- Record initial position and initial velocity.
- Record parameter values, units, time horizon, solver method, and step size.
- Test sensitivity to damping ratio, natural frequency, forcing amplitude, and forcing frequency.
- Explain whether oscillation is observed, inferred, idealized, or illustrative.
