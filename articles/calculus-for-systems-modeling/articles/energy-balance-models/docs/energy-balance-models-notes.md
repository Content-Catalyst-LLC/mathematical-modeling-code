# Energy Balance Models Notes

Energy balance models connect energy inputs, energy outputs, storage, heat capacity, radiation, albedo, forcing, feedback, equilibrium, transient response, thermal inertia, uncertainty, and claim boundaries.

## Review checklist

- Define system boundary, state variable, units, and time scale.
- Document incoming energy, outgoing energy, storage, heat capacity, forcing, feedback, albedo, and reservoir exchange.
- Separate equilibrium response from transient response.
- Preserve calibration data, uncertainty, sensitivity, omitted mechanisms, validation scope, and claim boundaries.
