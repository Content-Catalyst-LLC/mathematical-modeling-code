# Energy Balance Models

Advanced companion code and reproducible workflows for **Energy Balance Models** in the **Calculus for Systems Modeling** series.

## Themes

- energy stocks and flows
- conservation and energy imbalance
- heat capacity and thermal inertia
- incoming radiation, outgoing radiation, and albedo
- forcing, feedback, and equilibrium response
- one-layer and two-layer energy balance models
- ocean heat uptake and layered reservoirs
- surface energy partitioning
- building and infrastructure thermal balance
- sensitivity, uncertainty, calibration, and governance
- SQL governance registries
- Haskell typed energy balance records
- Catalyst Canvas governance
- reusable calculators

## Run

```bash
make smoke
make all
make calculators
make advanced
```
