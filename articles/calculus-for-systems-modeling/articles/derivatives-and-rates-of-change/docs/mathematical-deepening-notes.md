# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- average rate versus instantaneous rate;
- derivative as difference-quotient limit;
- derivative as local linear map;
- derivative with respect to time, state, parameter, control, or spatial coordinate;
- absolute rate versus relative rate;
- elasticity and positivity/domain conditions;
- one-sided derivative at boundaries;
- vector-valued rates and state-space motion;
- weak/distributional derivatives versus classical derivatives;
- symbolic derivative versus numerical derivative.

## Pathology checklist

- derivative does not exist at kinks;
- derivative estimates fail near discontinuities;
- elasticity unstable near zero;
- central differences invalid at constrained boundaries;
- finite differences suffer truncation and roundoff tradeoffs;
- rate interpretation fails when units are not stated;
- local derivative used as a global claim.
