Mathematical Deepening

This advanced layer treats derivatives as local approximation operators whose interpretation depends on domain, codomain, norm, variable of differentiation, units, and model context.

Formal Definitions

Derivative as Limit

The derivative is the limit of the difference quotient when the limit exists and is finite.

Derivative as Local Linear Map

Differentiability means \(f(a+h)=f(a)+f'(a)h+o(h)\). In higher dimensions the derivative is a linear map.

Elasticity

Elasticity \(E(x)=xf'(x)/f(x)\) is a dimensionless proportional sensitivity under positivity and domain assumptions.

Vector Field

A dynamic system \(dx/dt=F(x,t,\theta)\) interprets the derivative as governing state-space motion.
