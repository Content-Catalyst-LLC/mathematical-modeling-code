# Advanced Mathematical Audit: Derivatives and Rates of Change

## Formal topics included

- Average versus instantaneous rates
- Derivative as a difference-quotient limit
- Derivative as local linear map
- Units and dimensional interpretation
- Relative rates and elasticity
- Vector-field rates

## Numerical diagnostics included

- Forward and central finite differences
- Convergence-order estimates
- Roundoff and cancellation review
- Invariant-domain review

## Modeling implication

A derivative-based claim should specify what is changing, with respect to what, at which operating point, in which units, under which smoothness assumptions, and with what numerical evidence.
