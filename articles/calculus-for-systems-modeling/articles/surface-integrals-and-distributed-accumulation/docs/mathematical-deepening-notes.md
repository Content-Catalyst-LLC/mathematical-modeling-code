# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- scalar surface integral versus vector flux integral
- projected area versus actual surface area
- surface parameterization versus physical surface
- tangent vectors versus normal vectors
- normal direction versus flux sign
- open surface versus closed surface
- patch area versus mesh resolution
- boundary surface versus volume interior
- surface accumulation versus path accumulation
- scalar burden versus vector crossing

## Review checklist

- State the surface and boundary.
- Define the scalar or vector field.
- State whether the integral is scalar accumulation or flux.
- Document units for field values, coordinates, and surface area.
- Specify surface orientation and normal direction.
- Report parameter domain, mesh resolution, patch areas, and interpolation.
- Distinguish projected area from actual surface area.
- Explain whether the result represents burden, exposure, load, crossing, outflow, or inflow.
