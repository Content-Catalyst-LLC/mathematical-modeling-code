Mathematical Deepening

The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus formalizes the connection between local rates, accumulation functions, definite integrals, and endpoint differences. In systems modeling, it becomes a reconciliation principle for rates, states, baselines, intervals, and numerical residuals.

Formal FTC Conditions

FTC Part I

If f is continuous and A(x)=∫ from a to x of f(t) dt, then A'(x)=f(x).

FTC Part II

If F' = f, then ∫ from a to b of f(x) dx = F(b)-F(a).

Reconciliation

If Q'=r, endpoint difference should match accumulated rate over the same interval.

Residual Review

Numerical residuals can reveal grid error, unit mismatch, missing flows, or model inconsistency.
