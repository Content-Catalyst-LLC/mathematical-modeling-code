# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- circulation form versus flux form
- boundary integral versus area integral
- planar curl versus planar divergence
- closed curve versus enclosed region
- positive orientation versus reversed orientation
- line-integral approximation versus area-grid approximation
- boundary-region match versus boundary-region mismatch
- field definition versus theorem calculation
- symbolic theorem versus numerical audit
- local field behavior versus regional boundary behavior

## Review checklist

- Define the vector field and its units.
- State whether the circulation form or flux form is used.
- Confirm the boundary is closed and matched to the region.
- Document positive orientation and outward-normal convention.
- Report boundary sampling, interior grid resolution, and derivative method.
- Compare boundary-side and area-side estimates when possible.
