# Carbon Accumulation and Emissions Pathways Notes

Carbon accumulation models connect emissions flows, atmospheric stocks, land and ocean sinks, cumulative emissions, impulse response, carbon budgets, net zero, overshoot, negative emissions, uncertainty, and governance.

## Required distinctions

- annual emissions flow versus atmospheric carbon stock
- gross emissions versus net emissions
- removals versus offsets
- cumulative emissions versus current emissions
- fixed airborne fraction versus dynamic sinks
- budget estimate versus guarantee
- pathway scenario versus prediction
- net-zero target versus durable drawdown

## Review checklist

- Define accounting boundary, gas scope, time horizon, units, and baseline.
- Separate gross emissions, removals, and net emissions.
- Preserve emissions pathway records and cumulative totals.
- Document sink assumptions and carbon persistence structure.
- Record carbon-budget assumptions and probability framing.
- Document removal feasibility, durability, scale, monitoring, and governance.
- Attach uncertainty, sensitivity, validation scope, and claim boundaries.
