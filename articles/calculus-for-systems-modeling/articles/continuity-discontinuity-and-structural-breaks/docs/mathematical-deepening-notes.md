# Mathematical Deepening Notes

This article should be advanced enough for mathematicians. It includes real-analysis and numerical-diagnostic ideas beyond an introductory continuity explainer.

## Required distinctions

- pointwise continuity versus continuity on a set;
- domain/subspace continuity versus extension to an ambient space;
- one-sided continuity at boundaries;
- removable, jump, infinite, and essential discontinuity;
- structural break in level versus slope versus mechanism;
- uniform continuity versus pointwise continuity;
- Lipschitz continuity versus differentiability;
- absolute continuity versus continuity;
- semicontinuity for optimization;
- mathematical discontinuity versus noisy observed data.

## Pathology checklist

- continuous but not differentiable functions;
- everywhere discontinuous functions;
- pointwise limit of continuous functions that is discontinuous;
- continuous function not uniformly continuous on unbounded domain;
- numerical false positives for structural breaks;
- slope breaks without level jumps;
- level jumps without slope breaks.
