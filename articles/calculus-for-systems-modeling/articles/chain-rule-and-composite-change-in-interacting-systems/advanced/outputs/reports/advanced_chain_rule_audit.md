# Advanced Mathematical Audit: The Chain Rule and Composite Change in Interacting Systems

## Formal topics included

- Composite functions and domain compatibility
- Derivative as local linear map
- Chain rule as composition of derivative maps
- Jacobian composition
- Feedback-mediated direct and indirect terms
- Automatic-differentiation implementation warnings

## Modeling implication

A chain-rule claim should identify every pathway link, local derivative, domain condition, differentiability assumption, and whether the derivative describes a model, a program, or an empirically supported system pathway.
