Mathematical Deepening

The chain rule is a theorem about the composition of local approximations. If \(G\) is differentiable at \(a\) and \(F\) is differentiable at \(G(a)\), then \(D(F\circ G)(a)=DF(G(a))\circ DG(a)\).

Formal Chain-Rule Conditions

Domain Compatibility

The image of the inner function must lie in the domain of the outer function.

Differentiable Links

Every link in the pathway must be differentiable at the relevant point.

Jacobian Composition

In coordinates, derivative maps compose through Jacobian matrix multiplication.

Implementation Warning

Automatic differentiation gives derivatives of implemented code, not automatically derivatives of the real system.
