# Mathematical Deepening Notes

This article should be advanced enough for mathematicians. It includes real-analysis and numerical-analysis ideas that go beyond an introductory calculus explainer.

## Required distinctions

- limit point versus ordinary point;
- domain versus ambient space;
- codomain versus image/range;
- epsilon-delta versus sequential characterization;
- pointwise versus uniform convergence;
- one-sided versus two-sided limits;
- finite limit versus infinite/extended limit;
- mathematical convergence versus numerical approximation;
- mathematical validity versus empirical validity.

## Pathology checklist

- oscillatory functions with no limit;
- removable discontinuities;
- path-dependent multivariable limits;
- pointwise limits that fail to preserve continuity;
- noncommuting limits and operations;
- roundoff error for small finite-difference steps;
- boundary behavior on feasible sets.
