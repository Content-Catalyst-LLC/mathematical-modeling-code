# Advanced Mathematical Audit: Second Derivatives, Curvature, and Acceleration

## Formal topics included

- Twice differentiability
- Acceleration and change in rate
- Concavity and curvature
- Inflection candidate review
- Second-order approximation
- Critical-point interpretation
- Finite-difference stability
- Noise and smoothing warnings

## Modeling implication

A second-derivative result should state the function or trajectory, operating point, first derivative, second derivative, curvature interpretation, finite-difference method, smoothing assumptions, and noise warnings.
