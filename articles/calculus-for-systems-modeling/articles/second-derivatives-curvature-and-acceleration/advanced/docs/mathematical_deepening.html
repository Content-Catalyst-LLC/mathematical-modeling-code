Mathematical Deepening

Second derivatives require smoothness beyond first-derivative interpretation. They support acceleration, concavity, curvature, second-order approximation, and critical-point classification, but they are sensitive to noise and local validity limits.

Formal Second-Derivative Conditions

Twice Differentiability

The derivative must itself be differentiable at the point of second-derivative interpretation.

Concavity

The sign of the second derivative identifies local bending when smoothness and interval assumptions hold.

Curvature

Curvature incorporates both slope and second derivative to measure local bending.

Numerical Robustness

Second derivatives amplify noise and require step-size and smoothing review.
