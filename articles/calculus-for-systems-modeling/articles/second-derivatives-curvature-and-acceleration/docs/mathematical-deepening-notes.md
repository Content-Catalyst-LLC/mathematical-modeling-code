# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- first derivative versus second derivative;
- velocity versus acceleration;
- slope versus curvature;
- concavity versus trend direction;
- curvature versus second derivative alone;
- inflection candidate versus confirmed inflection;
- first-order approximation versus second-order approximation;
- smooth analytic curvature versus noisy numerical estimates.

## Pathology checklist

- nonsmooth function;
- second derivative not defined;
- inflection claimed only because f'' = 0;
- sign change not checked;
- noisy finite-difference acceleration;
- smoothing assumption not documented;
- curvature overinterpreted as tipping point;
- local second-order approximation extended too far.
