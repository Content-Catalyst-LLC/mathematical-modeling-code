Mathematical Deepening

Substitution is a one-dimensional change-of-variables theorem. In systems modeling, it is a preservation rule for accumulated meaning under transformed variables, scales, clocks, coordinates, and state representations.

Formal Substitution Conditions

Mapping

The transformed variable must be defined through a differentiable mapping.

Scale Factor

The derivative of the mapping converts differentials between variables.

Bounds

Definite integrals require transformed lower and upper bounds.

Monotonicity

Nonmonotonic transformations may require piecewise decomposition or richer state description.
