# Climate Feedback Models Notes

Climate feedback models connect energy balance, radiative forcing, heat capacity, feedback strength, ocean heat uptake, carbon-cycle response, equilibrium sensitivity, transient response, uncertainty, time scale, and governance.

## Required distinctions

- forcing versus feedback
- equilibrium response versus transient response
- restoring-positive convention versus climate-feedback sign convention
- one-box model versus two-box model
- simplified feedback model versus complete Earth-system model
- scenario output versus prediction
- uncertainty range versus single deterministic estimate

## Review checklist

- Define state variables, forcing assumptions, and time units.
- Document feedback sign convention explicitly.
- Preserve parameter records for forcing, feedback strength, heat capacity, ocean uptake, carbon-cycle feedback, and thresholds.
- Separate equilibrium climate sensitivity from transient response.
- Run sensitivity checks for forcing, feedback strength, and heat uptake.
- Attach uncertainty, validation scope, and claim boundaries to all outputs.
