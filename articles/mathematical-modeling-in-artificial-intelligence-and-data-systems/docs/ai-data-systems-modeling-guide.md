# AI and Data Systems Modeling Guide

## Article

**Mathematical Modeling in Artificial Intelligence and Data Systems**

## Central claim

AI systems are mathematical models embedded in data systems. They require data provenance, feature and label review, training objective documentation, validation, calibration, drift monitoring, subgroup diagnostics, privacy review, interpretability, and human accountability.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ai_model_register | Tracks model role, model family, data domain, decision context, and status |
| ai_model_candidate_review | Evaluates validation, calibration, subgroup error, drift, interpretability, privacy risk, and deployment criticality |
| ai_model_governance_card | Summarizes governance diagnostics and use limits |
| drift_monitoring_note | Defines post-deployment monitoring requirements |
| human_review_record | Documents decision authority, override, escalation, and contestability |
