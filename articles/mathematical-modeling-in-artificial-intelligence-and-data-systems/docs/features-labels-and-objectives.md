# Features, Labels, and Objectives

AI model behavior depends on features, labels, and training objectives.

## Review questions

- Are features valid for the intended decision?
- Do labels reflect ground truth, human judgment, or institutional history?
- Does the loss function match the real-world cost of error?
- Are thresholds transparent?
- Are constraints included for fairness, safety, privacy, or governance?

## Principle

Optimization only improves what the objective defines.
