# AI Fairness, Privacy, and Accountability

AI systems can influence access, visibility, opportunity, work, safety, and institutional treatment.

## Ethical risks

- false objectivity;
- disparate harm;
- privacy loss;
- opacity;
- automation bias;
- accountability gaps.

## Responsible practice

- audit labels and features;
- test subgroup performance;
- review privacy and security;
- document use limits;
- preserve human review and override;
- assign model owner and decision owner.
