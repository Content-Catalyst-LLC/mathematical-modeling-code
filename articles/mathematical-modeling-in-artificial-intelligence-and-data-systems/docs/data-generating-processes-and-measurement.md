# Data-Generating Processes and Measurement

AI models learn from records created by institutions, sensors, workflows, users, incentives, and histories.

## Review questions

- Why do these data exist?
- Who is represented and who is missing?
- How were labels assigned?
- What proxies are being used?
- What historical decisions are encoded?
- Could deployment differ from training data?

## Principle

A dataset is not reality. It is a measured and structured artifact of a data-generating process.
