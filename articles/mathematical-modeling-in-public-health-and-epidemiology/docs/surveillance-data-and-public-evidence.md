# Surveillance Data and Public Evidence

Public health models depend on surveillance systems that are incomplete and institutionally shaped.

## Review questions

- Are reported cases likely to underestimate true infections?
- Have testing access, definitions, or reporting delays changed?
- Are hospitalization and death data lagged?
- Are wastewater, syndromic, genomic, or serology signals available?
- Which groups or places are less visible in the data?

## Principle

Surveillance data must be interpreted as evidence generated by systems, not as a perfect mirror of disease reality.
