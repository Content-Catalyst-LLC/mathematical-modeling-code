# Public Health Modeling Ethics

Public health models can influence restrictions, resource allocation, surveillance, communication, and public trust.

## Ethical risks

- false certainty;
- unequal burden;
- data privacy failure;
- automation bias;
- stigmatization;
- accountability gaps.

## Responsible practice

- communicate scenarios and uncertainty;
- separate model evidence from decision authority;
- protect privacy;
- review equity and distribution;
- preserve challenge and accountability pathways;
- state use limits clearly.
