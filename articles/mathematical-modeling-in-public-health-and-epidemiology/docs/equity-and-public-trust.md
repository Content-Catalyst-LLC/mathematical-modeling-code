# Equity and Public Trust

Public health modeling has equity implications.

## Review questions

- Who has higher exposure?
- Who has higher severity risk?
- Who is undercounted in surveillance?
- Who has less access to testing, vaccination, treatment, or care?
- Does the model perform differently across groups?
- Can affected communities understand and contest model use?

## Principle

Equity is part of public health model validity, not an optional add-on.
