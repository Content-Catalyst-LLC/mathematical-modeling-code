# Public Health and Epidemiology Modeling Guide

## Article

**Mathematical Modeling in Public Health and Epidemiology**

## Central claim

Public health models are population-state and decision-support representations that connect transmission, surveillance, interventions, health-system demand, uncertainty, equity, communication, and public accountability.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| public_health_model_register | Tracks domain, model role, model family, public health question, and status |
| epidemic_scenario_review | Compares SIR intervention scenarios, capacity margins, and review classes |
| baseline_sir_trajectory | Provides reproducible susceptible, infectious, recovered, and hospital demand trajectories |
| public_health_model_review_card | Summarizes evidence, diagnostics, assumptions, and use limits |
| governance_record | Documents surveillance review, equity review, communication, and decision accountability |
