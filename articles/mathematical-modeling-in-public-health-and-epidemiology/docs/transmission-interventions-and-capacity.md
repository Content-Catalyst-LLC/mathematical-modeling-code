# Transmission, Interventions, and Capacity

Epidemiological models connect transmission dynamics to intervention choices and health-system demand.

## Review targets

- contact rate and transmission probability;
- recovery or removal rate;
- intervention timing and adherence;
- vaccination or immunity assumptions;
- peak infectious burden;
- hospitalization rate and length of stay;
- operational capacity;
- capacity breach risk.

## Principle

Capacity planning should include operational constraints, not just nominal beds or equipment.
