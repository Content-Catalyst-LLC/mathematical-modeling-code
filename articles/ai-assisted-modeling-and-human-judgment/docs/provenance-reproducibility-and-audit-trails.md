# Provenance, Reproducibility, and Audit Trails

AI assistance should leave a trace.

## Review questions

- What data were used?
- What prompts or instructions were used?
- Which assumptions were AI-generated, human-generated, or jointly revised?
- Which code was generated?
- Which outputs were reviewed?
- Who approved or rejected the model artifact?
- What use limits apply?

## Principle

A model that cannot be audited should not be trusted for consequential use.
