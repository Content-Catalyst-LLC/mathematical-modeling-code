# Automation Bias and False Authority

Automation bias occurs when people give too much weight to system-generated outputs.

## Risk signals

- polished but unverified summaries;
- generated code without tests;
- generated assumptions without evidence;
- dashboards that hide uncertainty;
- exploratory outputs used as decision evidence;
- unclear model or decision owner.

## Principle

AI-generated artifacts should be status-labeled: exploratory, draft, checked, validated, approved, or retired.
