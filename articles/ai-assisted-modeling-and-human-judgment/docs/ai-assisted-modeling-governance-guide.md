# AI-Assisted Modeling Governance Guide

## Article

**AI-Assisted Modeling and Human Judgment**

## Central claim

AI can assist modeling, but it cannot own purpose, evidence, interpretation, values, consequences, or decisions. Human judgment remains responsible for use.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ai_assistance_register | Tracks where AI assistance was used, what artifact was created, and whether provenance/human review are required |
| human_judgment_review | Scores judgment risk across evidence, uncertainty, consequences, automation bias, and accountability clarity |
| ai_assisted_modeling_governance_card | Summarizes AI use, human judgment risk, escalation needs, use limits, and ownership |
| provenance_record | Preserves data, assumptions, prompts, code, outputs, and review notes |
| use_limit_statement | Defines where AI-assisted outputs can and cannot be used |
