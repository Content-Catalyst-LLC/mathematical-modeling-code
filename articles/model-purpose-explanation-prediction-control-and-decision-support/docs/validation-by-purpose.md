# Validation by Purpose

## Explanation

Evidence needed:

- theory or mechanism review;
- qualitative behavior checks;
- domain expert review;
- comparison with alternative explanations.

## Prediction

Evidence needed:

- out-of-sample validation;
- forecast error reporting;
- calibration assessment;
- monitoring and update plan.

## Control

Evidence needed:

- stability and robustness analysis;
- stress testing;
- feedback and state estimation review;
- fail-safe and rollback rules.

## Decision support

Evidence needed:

- alternatives and consequences;
- uncertainty and sensitivity analysis;
- trade-off transparency;
- stakeholder or domain review;
- accountability and governance process.
