# Differential Equations for Systems Modeling: Notes

Differential equations provide a formal language for representing systems whose behavior depends on rates of change.

## Core Modeling Questions

- What are the state variables?
- What determines the rate of change?
- What initial conditions define the trajectory?
- What parameters shape the system?
- Are equations continuous, discrete, deterministic, stochastic, or hybrid?
- Does the model include feedback, forcing, coupling, delay, or thresholds?
- Are numerical results sensitive to time step, solver method, or parameter choice?

## Interpretation Cautions

- A smooth trajectory can hide shocks or discontinuities.
- Equilibrium does not guarantee resilience.
- Solver output is not automatically model truth.
- Parameter uncertainty can dominate conclusions.
- A simple ODE may miss spatial structure, network effects, stochastic events, or institutional constraints.
