# Robustness and Fragility Guide

## Article

**Robustness, Fragility, and Model Dependence**

## Central claim

Strong models reveal their dependencies. Robustness shows where conclusions survive plausible disturbance. Fragility shows where small changes can reverse interpretation or action. Model dependence shows where results rely on one representation, dataset, metric, threshold, or scenario.

## Required records

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| robustness_matrix | Crosses model forms and scenarios |
| fragility_ranking | Orders cases by threshold proximity |
| robustness_register | Tracks dependence layers and review questions |
| threshold_disagreement_note | Preserves action reversal evidence |
| use_limit_statement | Communicates where the result should not travel |
