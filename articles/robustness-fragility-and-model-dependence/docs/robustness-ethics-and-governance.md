# Robustness Ethics and Governance

Fragile conclusions have ethical stakes because they can influence public communication, safety rules, resource allocation, and policy priorities.

## Ethical risks

- false confidence;
- hidden dependence;
- suppressed model disagreement;
- selective robustness checks;
- adverse scenarios omitted;
- threshold harm;
- uneven risk burden.

## Responsible practice

- publish dependence registers;
- include adverse and stress scenarios;
- preserve model disagreement;
- report threshold fragility;
- state use limits;
- connect robustness to decisions and affected stakeholders.
