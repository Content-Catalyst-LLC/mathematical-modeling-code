# Distance and Accessibility Review

## Review questions

- Is distance Euclidean, network-based, time-based, cost-based, or perceived?
- Does the chosen distance metric match the phenomenon?
- Are barriers, routes, capacity, or travel modes relevant?
- Are buffers or thresholds justified?
- Does accessibility account for service capacity?
- Are accessibility outputs tested against alternative distance metrics?

## Principle

Distance is a model choice. Straight-line distance is often useful as a baseline, but it should not replace travel time, cost, barriers, or network structure when those are central to the question.
