# Complexity Modeling Guide

## Article

**Mathematical Modeling in an Age of Complexity**

## Central claim

Complex systems require models that support learning, comparison, adaptation, and accountable judgment under uncertainty rather than pretending that one model can fully predict or control the system.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| complexity_model_register | Tracks model role, family, complexity feature, decision context, and review status |
| complexity_scenario_review | Compares stress, interdependence, uncertainty, resilience, equity, adaptability, fragility, and robust value |
| complexity_model_review_card | Documents scenario review, adaptive triggers, model plurality, and governance use limits |
| adaptive_trigger_record | Defines signals that should change decisions |
| model_portfolio_note | Explains why multiple models are required |
