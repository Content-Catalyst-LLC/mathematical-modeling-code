# Complexity Ethics and Governance

Complexity modeling can inform high-consequence decisions while uncertainty remains unresolved.

## Ethical risks

- hidden values;
- unequal consequences;
- false certainty;
- responsibility diffusion;
- technocratic overreach;
- delayed harm.

## Responsible practice

- document assumptions and values;
- compare multiple models and scenarios;
- communicate uncertainty;
- preserve stakeholder review;
- assign model and decision owners;
- monitor, revise, and retire models when evidence changes.
