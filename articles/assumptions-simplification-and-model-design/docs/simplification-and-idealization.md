# Simplification and Idealization

## Simplification

Simplification removes detail to make the model tractable, interpretable, or computable.

## Idealization

Idealization knowingly introduces assumptions that are not literally true but may be useful for isolating structure.

## Approximation

Approximation represents a complex relationship or solution with a simpler numerical or analytical form.

## Distortion

Distortion occurs when simplification or idealization misleads interpretation for the intended use.

## Review questions

- What was removed?
- Why was it removed?
- What structure was preserved?
- What risk is created by the omission?
- When must the omitted detail be restored?
- Does the simplification still serve the model’s purpose?
