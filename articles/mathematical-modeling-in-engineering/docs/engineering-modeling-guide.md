# Engineering Modeling Guide

## Article

**Mathematical Modeling in Engineering**

## Central claim

Engineering models are design-constrained representations that connect requirements, physics, materials, constraints, safety margins, uncertainty, validation, and responsible technical judgment.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| engineering_model_register | Tracks domain, role, model family, design question, and status |
| beam_design_review | Compares design alternatives by stress, margin, mass, and safety factor |
| engineering_design_review_card | Summarizes design evidence, failed constraints, safety review, and use limits |
| validation_domain_note | Prevents model outputs from traveling beyond tested engineering use |
| design_constraint_register | Documents design requirements and acceptance criteria |
