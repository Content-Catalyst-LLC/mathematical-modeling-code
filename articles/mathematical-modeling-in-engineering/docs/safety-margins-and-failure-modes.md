# Safety Margins and Failure Modes

Engineering design must consider failure, not only expected performance.

## Review targets

- load cases;
- capacity and resistance;
- safety factor;
- failure probability;
- fatigue, buckling, overheating, overload, instability, corrosion, or wear;
- consequence of failure;
- inspection and monitoring needs.

## Principle

A model that predicts ordinary performance but omits failure modes is incomplete for many engineering decisions.
