# Engineering Modeling Ethics

Engineering models can affect safety, infrastructure, resource use, accessibility, public trust, and environmental outcomes.

## Ethical risks

- false safety;
- hidden tradeoffs;
- misuse outside validated conditions;
- irreproducible design reasoning;
- unequal burden of failure;
- accountability gaps.

## Responsible practice

- document assumptions and constraints;
- compute and communicate margins;
- validate for purpose;
- preserve workflow artifacts;
- state use limits;
- assign decision ownership.
