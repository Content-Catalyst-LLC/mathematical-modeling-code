# Requirements, Constraints, and Design Intent

Engineering models are shaped by requirements and constraints.

## Required questions

- What function must the design perform?
- What loads, environments, and use cases are expected?
- What constraints are hard requirements?
- Which requirements are tradeoff variables?
- What acceptance criteria determine adequacy?
- What safety margins are required?

## Principle

A technically detailed model is incomplete if design requirements and constraints are unclear.
