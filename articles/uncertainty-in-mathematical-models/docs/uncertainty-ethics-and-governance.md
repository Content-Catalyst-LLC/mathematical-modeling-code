# Uncertainty Ethics and Governance

Uncertainty communication has ethical stakes because model outputs can influence decisions affecting people, resources, institutions, environments, and future risks.

## Ethical risks

- false precision;
- hidden structural uncertainty;
- unlabeled probability statements;
- selective uncertainty reporting;
- decision overreach;
- uneven risk burden;
- no use-limit statement;
- no monitoring plan.

## Responsible practice

- publish an uncertainty register;
- identify included and excluded uncertainty sources;
- report threshold risk;
- preserve structural uncertainty notes;
- communicate uncertainty in usable language;
- connect uncertainty to decision consequences.
