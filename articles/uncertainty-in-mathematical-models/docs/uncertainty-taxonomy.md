# Uncertainty Taxonomy

## Article

**Uncertainty in Mathematical Models**

## Central claim

Uncertainty is not a weakness to hide. It is evidence to classify, propagate, interpret, and communicate.

## Main uncertainty categories

| Category | Meaning | Responsible response |
|---|---|---|
| Data uncertainty | Observations are noisy, incomplete, biased, or delayed | Audit and propagate data quality |
| Input uncertainty | Inputs supplied to the model are uncertain | Use ranges, distributions, or scenarios |
| Parameter uncertainty | Parameter values are estimated or assumed | Use intervals, sensitivity analysis, or posterior distributions |
| Structural uncertainty | Model form may be incomplete or wrong | Compare structures and state model-form limits |
| Scenario uncertainty | Future conditions are uncertain | Use scenario reasoning and robustness checks |
| Decision uncertainty | Values, thresholds, or consequences are uncertain | Connect uncertainty to action and risk tolerance |
