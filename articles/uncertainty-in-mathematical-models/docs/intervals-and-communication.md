# Intervals and Communication

Intervals are not interchangeable.

## Common intervals

- confidence interval: uncertainty about an estimated quantity under a sampling framework;
- prediction interval: uncertainty about a future observation or outcome;
- credible interval: posterior uncertainty under Bayesian assumptions;
- scenario range: outputs under named assumptions;
- uncertainty envelope: range across selected model runs or assumptions.

## Communication rules

- State what the interval represents.
- State what it does not include.
- Avoid implying certainty with excessive decimal precision.
- Explain threshold probabilities when decisions are involved.
- State structural uncertainty separately when needed.
