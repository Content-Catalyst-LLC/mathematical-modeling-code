# Ethics of Units and Scale

Units and scale affect what becomes visible.

## Risks

- aggregate units hide local harm;
- per-capita units hide total burden;
- normalized scores hide original measurement meaning;
- short horizons hide accumulation;
- large spatial units hide neighborhood vulnerability;
- unit conversion errors produce misleading recommendations.

## Responsible practice

- report units in all outputs;
- use both aggregate and disaggregated diagnostics where relevant;
- explain normalized scores;
- test scale sensitivity;
- document conversion factors;
- avoid transferring conclusions beyond the model's valid scale.
