# Scale Review Plan

## Scale dimensions

- temporal scale;
- spatial scale;
- population scale;
- mechanism scale;
- numerical scale;
- decision scale.

## Review questions

- What scale was the model calibrated at?
- What scale is the model being interpreted at?
- Does aggregation hide important variation?
- Does changing the time step change conclusions?
- Are dimensionless quantities used for comparison across systems?
- Are results transferable outside the scale of design?

## Required artifacts

- unit register;
- conversion log;
- dimensional audit;
- scale statement;
- magnitude checks;
- use-limit note.
