# Omitted Detail Register

| Omitted detail | Why omitted | Risk | Possible extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Spatial variation | First model is aggregate | Local shortages may be hidden | Spatial model or network model |
| Water quality | Focus is quantity | Usable supply may be overstated | Quality threshold model |
| Legal allocation | Demonstration model | Rights and constraints may be ignored | Policy constraint layer |
| Ecological flows | Narrow resource framing | Environmental harms may be hidden | Minimum ecological flow constraint |
| Stochastic inflow | Deterministic transparency | Drought risk understated | Monte Carlo or ensemble inflow |
| Distributional effects | Aggregate output | Unequal burdens hidden | Subgroup output layer |
