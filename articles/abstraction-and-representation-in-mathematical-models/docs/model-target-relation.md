# Model-Target Relation

## Target system

The target system is the real-world or conceptual system the model is about.

## Model

The model is a formal representation of selected target-system features.

## Representation relation

A model may relate to its target through:

- structural similarity;
- mechanistic correspondence;
- analogy;
- calibration;
- statistical fit;
- causal assumptions;
- scenario-based exploration;
- expert/domain judgment.

## Adequacy

A representation is adequate only relative to:

- purpose;
- evidence;
- uncertainty;
- boundary;
- scale;
- consequences of use.

## Review checklist

- What does the model represent?
- What does it omit?
- Why is the chosen representation appropriate?
- What would break the representation?
- What uses should be prohibited?
