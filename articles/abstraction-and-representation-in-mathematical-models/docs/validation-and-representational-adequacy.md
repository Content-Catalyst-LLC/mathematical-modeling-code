# Validation and Representational Adequacy

## Validation question

Does the representation preserve enough relevant structure for its intended use?

## Evidence needed before operational use

- empirical stock/storage observations;
- inflow and outflow records;
- loss estimates;
- capacity and operating constraints;
- independent validation period;
- uncertainty analysis;
- expert/domain review.

## Adequacy statement template

This representation is adequate for: **[purpose]**  
It preserves: **[structure]**  
It omits: **[omissions]**  
It is supported by: **[evidence]**  
It is uncertain because: **[uncertainty]**  
It should not be used for: **[prohibited uses]**
