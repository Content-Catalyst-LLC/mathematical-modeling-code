# Ethical Representation Notes

Abstraction is not neutral. A model’s representation determines what becomes visible and what disappears.

## Ethical review questions

- Who or what is excluded from the model?
- Are harms aggregated away?
- Are proxies being treated as the underlying reality?
- Are uncertainty and omitted detail visible?
- Are affected communities represented only as variables?
- Does the objective function omit values that matter?
- Can users contest the representation?

## Responsible practice

- state boundaries;
- identify proxies;
- report uncertainty;
- show distributional effects where relevant;
- document omitted detail;
- separate model output from decision authority.
