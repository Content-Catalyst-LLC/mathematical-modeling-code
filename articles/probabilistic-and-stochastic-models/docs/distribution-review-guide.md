# Distribution Review Guide

## Core checks

- Does the distribution match the support of the variable?
- Are negative values impossible, truncated, or allowed?
- Is the distribution symmetric or skewed?
- Are tails plausible for the decision context?
- Are parameters estimated, assumed, or transferred?
- Are dependence assumptions documented?
- Are rare but severe outcomes represented?

## Common failures

- normal distribution by convenience;
- independence by default;
- mean-only reporting;
- tail risk hidden;
- probabilities reported without consequences;
- simulation count too small for tail estimates.
