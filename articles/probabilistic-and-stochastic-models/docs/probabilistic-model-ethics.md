# Ethics of Probabilistic Modeling

Probabilistic models can improve decision-making by showing uncertainty, but they can also hide responsibility when probabilities are misused.

## Risks

- low probability used to dismiss severe harm;
- expected values hide tail outcomes;
- aggregate probabilities hide subgroup risk;
- opaque priors encode hidden assumptions;
- independence assumptions underestimate joint risk;
- probability language is mistaken for certainty;
- simulation volume disguises weak assumptions.

## Responsible practice

- report probability and severity together;
- communicate tail risk;
- disaggregate where impacts differ;
- document distributions and priors;
- test dependence assumptions;
- validate calibration and interval coverage;
- frame outputs as conditional on assumptions.
