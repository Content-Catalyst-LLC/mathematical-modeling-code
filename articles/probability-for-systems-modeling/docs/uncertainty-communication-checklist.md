# Uncertainty Communication Checklist

## Before Reporting Results

- Identify the source of uncertainty.
- Document probability distributions and assumptions.
- Check whether outcomes are independent or dependent.
- Report more than a mean when tails matter.
- Include intervals, quantiles, or scenario ranges when appropriate.
- State whether probabilities are empirical, modeled, simulated, or judgment-based.
- Avoid implying certainty when evidence is limited.

## Useful Summaries

- Expected value
- Median
- Variance or standard deviation
- Quantiles
- Tail probabilities
- Probability of exceeding a threshold
- Sensitivity to assumptions
