# Probability for Systems Modeling: Notes

Probability provides a formal language for uncertainty, stochastic behavior, dependence, risk, and variation.

## Core Modeling Questions

- What uncertain outcomes are being represented?
- Are probabilities estimated from data, assumed, elicited, or simulated?
- What distributions are being used, and why?
- Are events independent, dependent, correlated, or conditionally related?
- Are rare events or tail outcomes important?
- How does uncertainty propagate through the model?
- How should results be interpreted and communicated?

## Interpretation Cautions

- Expected value can hide tail risk.
- Independence assumptions can fail in complex systems.
- Precise probabilities can create false confidence.
- Simulation outputs are only as credible as their inputs.
- Bayesian results can be sensitive to priors.
- Rare events may be underrepresented in small datasets.
