# Measurement, Calibration, and Validation

Measurement connects models to evidence, but measurements are not raw truth.

## Required questions

- What quantity is measured?
- What model variable does it correspond to?
- What measurement error or sampling bias is present?
- Which parameters are calibrated?
- What data are reserved for validation?
- What is the model's domain of validity?

## Principle

Calibration improves fit. Validation builds purpose-specific credibility. Neither proves the model true in an absolute sense.
