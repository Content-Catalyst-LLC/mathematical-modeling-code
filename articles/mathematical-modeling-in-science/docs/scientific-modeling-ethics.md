# Scientific Modeling Ethics

Scientific models can influence public knowledge, institutional decisions, environmental management, health guidance, risk perception, and policy.

## Ethical risks

- false certainty;
- hidden assumptions;
- irreproducible workflow;
- misuse outside domain of validity;
- suppressed uncertainty;
- scientific claims overstated beyond evidence.

## Responsible practice

- document assumptions;
- communicate uncertainty;
- publish workflow artifacts;
- state use limits;
- validate for purpose;
- preserve model disagreement where relevant.
