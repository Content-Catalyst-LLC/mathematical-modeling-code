# Theory, Observation, and Models

Scientific models mediate between theory and observation.

## Pattern

- Theory supplies a hypothesis, law, mechanism, or conceptual structure.
- The model formalizes the theory under assumptions.
- Observation constrains, calibrates, or challenges the model.
- Discrepancy guides revision of theory, data, model structure, or measurement.

## Principle

A scientific model should remain connected to the theory and evidence that give it meaning.
