# Feasible Region and Constraint Review

## Constraint types

- nonnegativity;
- capacity;
- budget;
- balance;
- safety threshold;
- domain restriction;
- policy or legal rule.

## Review questions

- What is the source of the constraint?
- Is it hard or soft?
- Is the constraint enforced in code?
- Is the constraint mathematically necessary or chosen by policy?
- Does a high-scoring scenario violate feasibility?
- Does constraint relaxation change ethical interpretation?
