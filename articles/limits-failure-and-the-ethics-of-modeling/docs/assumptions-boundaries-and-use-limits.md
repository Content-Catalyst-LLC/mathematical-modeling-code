# Assumptions, Boundaries, and Use Limits

Every model has boundaries. A use-limit statement prevents a model from drifting into decisions it was not designed or validated to support.

## Review questions

- What system boundary does the model use?
- What scale and time horizon are represented?
- Which variables, groups, mechanisms, or consequences are excluded?
- Which assumptions matter most?
- What decisions is the model not approved to support?

## Principle

A model's boundary is also an ethical boundary.
