# Model Governance and Retirement

Model governance is the practice that keeps models from becoming unreviewed authority.

## Governance artifacts

- model register;
- validation record;
- use-limit statement;
- equity and harm review;
- monitoring plan;
- incident response;
- ownership record;
- retirement criteria.

## Principle

Deployment is not the end of modeling. It is the beginning of monitoring, challenge, revision, and accountability.
