# Model Ethics and Failure Guide

## Article

**Limits, Failure, and the Ethics of Modeling**

## Central claim

Models are powerful because they simplify. They become dangerous when their assumptions, uncertainty, boundaries, data limits, validation limits, failure modes, equity effects, and accountability obligations are hidden.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| model_failure_register | Tracks failure mode, lifecycle stage, ethical issue, likely cause, and review status |
| model_ethics_risk_review | Scores severity, likelihood, detectability gap, uncertainty, equity concern, and accountability gap |
| model_ethics_governance_card | Summarizes failure modes, ethics risk, use limits, and decision ownership |
| governance_queue | Prioritizes failure modes for review or revision |
| use_limit_statement | Defines where the model can and cannot be used |
