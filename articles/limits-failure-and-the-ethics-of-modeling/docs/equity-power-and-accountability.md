# Equity, Power, and Accountability

Model ethics asks accurate for whom, harmful to whom, useful for whom, and accountable to whom.

## Review questions

- Who is represented and who is missing?
- Are errors distributed unevenly?
- Are proxies valid and fair?
- Can affected people challenge outcomes?
- Who owns the model?
- Who owns the decision?

## Principle

A model does not make a decision. People and institutions do.
