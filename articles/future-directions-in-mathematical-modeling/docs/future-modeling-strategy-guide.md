# Future Modeling Strategy Guide

Future-ready modeling is disciplined, plural, reproducible, uncertainty-aware, governed, and human-judgment-centered.

Required artifacts:

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| future_modeling_direction_register | Tracks future directions, maturity, governance need, uncertainty pressure, and human judgment need |
| future_modeling_review_card | Summarizes strategic priority, governance needs, uncertainty briefs, and human judgment protocols |
| model_lifecycle_note | Defines how models will be maintained, updated, monitored, revised, or retired |
| uncertainty_brief | Preserves uncertainty and scenario pressure |
| governance_plan | Assigns model owner, decision owner, review cycle, and use limits |
