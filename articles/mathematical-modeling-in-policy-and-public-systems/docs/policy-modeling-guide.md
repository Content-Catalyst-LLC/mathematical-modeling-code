# Policy Modeling Guide

## Article

**Mathematical Modeling in Policy and Public Systems**

## Central claim

Policy models are decision-support representations for public reasoning. They clarify options, assumptions, constraints, risks, uncertainty, distributional consequences, and governance obligations.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| policy_model_register | Tracks domain, role, model family, public question, and status |
| policy_option_review | Compares options by benefit, cost, feasibility, equity, risk, and uncertainty |
| policy_decision_support_card | Summarizes public evidence, diagnostics, and use limits |
| equity_review_note | Documents distributional implications |
| governance_record | Documents model owner, decision owner, update triggers, and challenge pathway |
