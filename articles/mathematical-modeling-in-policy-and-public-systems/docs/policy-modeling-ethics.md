# Policy Modeling Ethics

Policy models can influence public resources, access, burden, risk exposure, rights, and institutional trust.

## Ethical risks

- false neutrality;
- distributional harm;
- automation bias;
- public opacity;
- overreach outside approved use;
- accountability gaps.

## Responsible practice

- document objectives and assumptions;
- review distribution;
- communicate uncertainty;
- separate model evidence from decision authority;
- preserve challenge pathways;
- assign accountability.
