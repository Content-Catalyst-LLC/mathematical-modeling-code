# Model Interpretation Guide

## Article

**Model Interpretation and Decision-Making**

## Central claim

A model output is evidence. A decision is a commitment to action. Interpretation is the governed bridge between the two.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| interpretation_register | Tracks output meaning, uncertainty, thresholds, values, and governance |
| decision_option_review | Compares options with uncertainty, burden, consequence, and threshold risk |
| decision_support_review_card | Summarizes decision evidence, fragility, and use limits |
| threshold_review | Connects uncertainty to action boundaries |
| governance_record | Documents ownership, monitoring, and update obligations |
