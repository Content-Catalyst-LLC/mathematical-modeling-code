# Tradeoffs, Values, and Objectives

Decision models often encode values through objective functions, constraints, weights, penalties, and excluded variables.

## Required questions

- What objective is being optimized?
- What constraints are imposed?
- What values are represented?
- What values are excluded?
- Who bears the cost of error?
- Are affected stakeholders visible?

## Principle

Good interpretation does not hide value judgments behind model outputs.
