# Model Interpretation Ethics

Model interpretation has ethical stakes because model-supported decisions can affect access, safety, exposure, opportunity, resources, rights, and public trust.

## Ethical risks

- automation bias;
- decision laundering;
- hidden values;
- unequal impact;
- ignored uncertainty;
- no appeal pathway;
- no accountable owner.

## Responsible practice

- separate output from recommendation;
- communicate uncertainty and threshold risk;
- document values and tradeoffs;
- review subgroup effects;
- assign decision ownership;
- monitor and revise.
