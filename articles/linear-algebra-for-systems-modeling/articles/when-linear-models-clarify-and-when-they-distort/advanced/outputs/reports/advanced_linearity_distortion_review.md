# Advanced Linearity Distortion Review

- **linearity_assumption** (required): Review additivity, proportionality, superposition, and coefficient stability.
- **local_vs_global_validity** (required): State whether the linear model is local, global, exploratory, predictive, explanatory, or diagnostic.
- **residual_structure** (required): Inspect residuals for curvature, thresholds, changing variance, subgroup differences, and time dependence.
- **interaction_review** (required): Check whether variables modify each other's effects rather than contributing independently.
- **threshold_and_regime_review** (required): Review saturation, capacity limits, discontinuities, and regime changes.
- **feedback_review** (required): Assess whether outputs influence future inputs or whether the transition matrix changes with state.
- **aggregation_review** (required): Determine whether averages hide subgroup, spatial, temporal, or network heterogeneity.
- **extrapolation_review** (required): Flag predictions and scenarios outside observed or validated ranges.
- **causal_interpretation** (required): Separate prediction, association, mechanism, and causal identification.
- **responsible_communication** (required): Explain what the linear model clarifies, what it omits, and where distortion risk is highest.
