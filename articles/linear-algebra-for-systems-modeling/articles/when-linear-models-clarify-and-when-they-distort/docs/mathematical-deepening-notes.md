# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- linearity versus affine form
- additivity versus interaction
- proportionality versus saturation
- local linear approximation versus global validity
- residual minimization versus model adequacy
- predictive association versus causal interpretation
- average effect versus subgroup behavior
- extrapolation versus interpolation
- computational fit versus responsible interpretation

## Review checklist

- Document model purpose, operating range, linearity assumptions, additivity assumptions, proportionality assumptions, and coefficient stability.
- Inspect residuals for curvature, thresholds, changing variance, subgroup patterns, and time dependence.
- Compare linear, transformed, piecewise, interaction, and nonlinear alternatives when conclusions matter.
- Flag extrapolated outputs and scenarios outside the observed or validated range.
- Preserve assumption registers, diagnostics, validation evidence, uncertainty, and interpretation boundaries in generated outputs.
