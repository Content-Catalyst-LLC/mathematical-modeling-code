# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- transactions matrix versus technical coefficient matrix
- intermediate demand versus final demand
- direct requirements versus indirect requirements
- total output response versus causal proof
- multipliers as summaries versus complete explanations
- national tables versus regional or firm-level supply chains
- demand-driven scenario analysis versus supply-constrained disruption
- sector aggregation versus operational bottlenecks
- environmental extension coefficients versus full responsibility claims

## Review checklist

- Define sectors, classification system, year, region, valuation basis, and source.
- Document transactions matrix orientation: supplier rows and using-sector columns.
- Document technical coefficient construction and zero-output handling.
- Report final demand vector, total output vector, and scenario shock vector.
- Check invertibility and conditioning of net requirements matrix.
- Report multipliers and environmental extensions with assumptions.
- Run sensitivity tests for coefficients, final demand, sector aggregation, and boundary choices.
