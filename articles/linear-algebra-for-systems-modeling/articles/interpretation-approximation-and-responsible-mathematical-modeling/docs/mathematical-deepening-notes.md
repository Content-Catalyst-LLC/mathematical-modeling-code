# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- mathematical result versus responsible claim
- approximation error versus numerical error
- validation evidence versus model assumption
- prediction versus explanation versus causality
- representation sensitivity versus numerical sensitivity
- model output versus decision authority
- reproducibility versus validity
- precision versus trustworthiness
- computation versus governance

## Review checklist

- Document model purpose, claim type, approximation form, evidence boundary, and interpretation boundary.
- Separate approximation error, data uncertainty, numerical uncertainty, sampling uncertainty, and interpretive uncertainty.
- Preserve residual diagnostics, condition checks, rank diagnostics, sensitivity tests, and validation evidence.
- State whether outputs are exploratory, descriptive, predictive, explanatory, causal, optimization-based, or governance-supporting.
- Include responsible-use statements and stop-use conditions in generated reports.
