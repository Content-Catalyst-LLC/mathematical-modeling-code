# Advanced Network Flow Review

- **flow_quantity** (required): Define what moves, with units and time scale.
- **source_sink_definition** (required): Document supply, demand, source, sink, and boundary choices.
- **capacity_definition** (required): Define edge capacities and review uncertainty or time variation.
- **conservation_assumption** (required): State whether nodes conserve, store, lose, transform, queue, or delay flow.
- **cost_semantics** (required): Document whether costs mean distance, time, money, risk, emissions, or loss.
- **cut_bottleneck_review** (recommended): Review cuts, saturated edges, bottlenecks, and vulnerability surfaces.
- **sensitivity_testing** (recommended): Test capacity, cost, demand, boundary, and edge-failure perturbations.
- **equity_and_burden** (required): Review who benefits from or bears access, delay, cost, and vulnerability tradeoffs.
