# Advanced Linear Algebra Modeling Audit

## State Vector

- Question: What does each vector component represent?
- Warning: Missing units or ordering can invalidate interpretation.

## Matrix Semantics

- Question: What do matrix entries mean?
- Warning: A valid operation may be meaningless if entries are not semantically defined.

## Rank and Dependency

- Question: Is structure independent, redundant, or underdetermined?
- Warning: Rank is a diagnostic, not a substitute for model judgment.

## Eigenvalue Claims

- Question: Is stability or long-run behavior being inferred correctly?
- Warning: Eigenstructure depends on model form, scaling, and linearity assumptions.

## Reduction Loss

- Question: What does dimensionality reduction preserve or discard?
- Warning: Clean components can hide important variation.
