# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- transactions matrix versus technical coefficient matrix
- final demand vector versus total output vector
- net requirements matrix versus Leontief inverse
- direct dependence versus indirect dependence
- productivity condition versus numeric invertibility
- multiplier summary versus causal proof
- demand-driven scenario versus supply-constrained disruption
- sector-level dependence versus firm-level supply-chain evidence
- environmental coefficients versus full responsibility claims

## Review checklist

- Define sectors, classification system, region, year, valuation basis, and source.
- Document coefficient construction, orientation, imports, and zero-output handling.
- Check productivity conditions, spectral radius, invertibility, and condition number.
- Report the Leontief inverse, multipliers, shock responses, and environmental extensions.
- Test sensitivity to coefficients, final demand, aggregation, boundary, and extension coefficients.
- Interpret results as structured estimates under fixed-coefficient assumptions.
