# Advanced Rank-Nullity Review

- **rank_interpretation** (required): Interpret rank as independent structure, not model adequacy.
- **nullity_interpretation** (required): Interpret nullity as freedom, non-identifiability, or missing constraints.
- **row_dependence_review** (required): Review dependent rows as redundant or meaningful constraints.
- **column_dependence_review** (required): Review dependent columns as overlapping variables or features.
- **rank_deficiency_review** (required): Explain whether rank deficiency is expected, useful, or problematic.
- **numerical_rank_tolerance** (required): Document tolerance and test sensitivity of numerical rank decisions.
