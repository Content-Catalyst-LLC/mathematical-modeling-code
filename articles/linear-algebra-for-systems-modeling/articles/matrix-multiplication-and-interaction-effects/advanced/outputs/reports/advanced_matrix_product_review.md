# Advanced Matrix Product Review

- **dimension_compatibility** (required): Check inner dimensions and intermediate-layer meaning.
- **composition_order** (required): Document what acts first and what acts second.
- **row_column_interpretation** (required): Explain product entries as dot products or pathway aggregations.
- **unit_index_alignment** (required): Review units, row/column order, and naming conventions.
- **noncommutativity_review** (required): Do not reverse matrix order without a valid process interpretation.
- **indirect_effect_validation** (recommended): Validate mediated pathways before treating product entries as effects.
