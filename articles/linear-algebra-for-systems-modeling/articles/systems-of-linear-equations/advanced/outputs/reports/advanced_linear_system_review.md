# Advanced Linear System Review

- **equation_meaning** (required): Document the real-world meaning of every equation row.
- **variable_meaning** (required): Document each unknown and its feasible range.
- **rhs_targets** (required): Record whether right-hand-side values are targets, observations, or balances.
- **rank_consistency** (required): Compare coefficient rank and augmented rank.
- **feasibility_review** (required): Review nonnegativity, capacity, legal, ethical, and operational constraints.
