# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- vector space versus geometric vector space
- inner product versus dot product
- norm versus distance
- Euclidean norm versus weighted norm
- raw units versus normalized coordinates
- residual vector versus residual norm
- cosine similarity versus Euclidean distance
- covariance-aware distance versus raw coordinate distance
- numerical norm versus substantive model meaning

## Review checklist

- Document coordinate units and scaling.
- State the inner product and norm.
- Report multiple norm values where interpretation depends on geometry.
- Document weights and covariance assumptions.
- Translate normalized or weighted results back into domain terms.
- Report residual norms and condition diagnostics.
