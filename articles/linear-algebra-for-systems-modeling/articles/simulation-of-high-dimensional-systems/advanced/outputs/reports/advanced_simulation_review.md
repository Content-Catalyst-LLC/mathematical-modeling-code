# Advanced Simulation of High-Dimensional Systems Review

- **state_vector** (required): Define modeled variables, state dimension, units, resolution, and system boundary.
- **transition_rule** (required): Document update equations, sparse structure, time step, external inputs, and stability diagnostics.
- **parameter_space** (required): Document parameter ranges, baseline assumptions, stress scenarios, and intervention scenarios.
- **uncertainty_model** (required): Document random input distributions, covariance assumptions, shock generation, and random seeds.
- **ensemble_design** (required): Report ensemble size, time horizon, summary statistics, quantiles, extremes, and threshold exceedance.
- **dimensionality_management** (required): Review sparsity, projections, reduced-order methods, compression, and information-loss diagnostics.
- **validation_design** (required): Compare simulation behavior with observed data, known mechanisms, numerical checks, and domain review.
- **responsible_use** (required): Present simulations as conditional model outcomes, not observations of the future or certainty.
