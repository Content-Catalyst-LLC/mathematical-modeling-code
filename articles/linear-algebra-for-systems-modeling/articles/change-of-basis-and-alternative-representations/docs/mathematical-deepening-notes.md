# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- vector versus coordinate representation
- basis matrix versus transformation matrix
- basis coordinates versus standard coordinates
- coordinate conversion versus coordinate recovery
- matrix entries versus underlying transformation
- similarity transformation versus equality
- invariant property versus representation artifact
- valid basis versus well-conditioned basis
- numerical convenience versus domain interpretability

## Review checklist

- Document the original coordinate system.
- Record the proposed alternative basis.
- Check full rank and determinant when applicable.
- Compute reconstruction error.
- Check conditioning or warn when not computed.
- Transform matrices through similarity when appropriate.
- Preserve invariant checks where possible.
- Translate transformed results back into domain terms.
