# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- sector-level dependency versus asset-level dependency
- dependency matrix versus coupling matrix
- service-level vector versus physical capacity
- one-step cascade estimate versus validated failure prediction
- redundancy on paper versus usable redundancy
- recovery priority vector versus public decision authority
- dependency burden versus actual social consequence
- aggregate service loss versus distributional impact
- scenario exploration versus operational forecast

## Review checklist

- Document sector semantics, dependency semantics, weight evidence, scenario scope, and time horizon.
- Preserve dependency matrices, coupling matrices, service vectors, initial shocks, cascade estimates, and sensitivity variants.
- Compare results across alternative dependency weights, redundancy assumptions, delays, recovery capacities, and boundary choices.
- Attach uncertainty flags to weights, missing dependencies, confidential data gaps, outdated records, and judgment-based assumptions.
- State interpretation boundaries, equity considerations, validation evidence, and stop-use conditions.
