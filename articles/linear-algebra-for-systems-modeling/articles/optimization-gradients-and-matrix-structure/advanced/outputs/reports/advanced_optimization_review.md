# Advanced Optimization, Gradients, and Matrix Structure Review

- **decision_variables** (required): Define controllable variables, units, bounds, and relationship to real decision authority.
- **objective_function** (required): Document what is minimized or maximized and whether the objective is a valid proxy for the system goal.
- **constraint_matrix** (required): Document equality, inequality, feasibility, capacity, safety, policy, and ethical constraints.
- **gradient_formula** (required): Report gradient expression, update rule, step size, and final gradient norm.
- **curvature_diagnostics** (required): Report Hessian structure, eigenvalue or singular-value review, convexity, and conditioning.
- **solver_convergence** (required): Document solver method, stopping rules, objective history, and convergence status.
- **sensitivity_testing** (required): Test objective weights, constraints, data perturbations, regularization strength, and parameter uncertainty.
- **responsible_use** (required): Present the optimum as a model result under assumptions, not automatic policy, causality, or truth.
