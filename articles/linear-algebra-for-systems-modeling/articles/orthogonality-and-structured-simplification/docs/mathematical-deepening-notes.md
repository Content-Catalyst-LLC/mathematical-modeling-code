# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- orthogonality versus linear independence
- zero dot product versus real-world independence
- orthogonal basis versus orthonormal basis
- standard dot product versus weighted inner product
- projection residual versus unexplained noise
- Gram-Schmidt versus stable QR workflows
- normal equations versus QR/SVD least-squares solvers
- numerical orthogonality versus exact symbolic orthogonality

## Review checklist

- Record vectors or matrix columns being tested.
- State the inner product or geometry.
- Record dot products and tolerance.
- Compute normalized directions where appropriate.
- Check projection residuals.
- Report orthonormality error such as ||Q^T Q - I||.
- Include rank and conditioning warnings.
- Interpret residuals substantively.
