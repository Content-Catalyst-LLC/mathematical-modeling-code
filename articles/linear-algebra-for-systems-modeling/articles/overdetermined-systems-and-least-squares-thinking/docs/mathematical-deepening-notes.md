# Mathematical Deepening Notes

## Required distinctions

- exact solution versus least-squares approximation
- residual vector versus residual norm
- best fit versus truth
- small residual versus model validity
- normal equations versus stable numerical solution
- full column rank versus coefficient interpretability
- regression coefficient versus causal effect
- weighted error versus unweighted error
- approximation criterion versus decision criterion

## Review checklist

- Record matrix shape and whether m > n.
- Record row meanings and column meanings.
- Compute rank and review identifiability.
- Report solution, fitted values, residual vector, and residual norm.
- Document solver method and conditioning concern.
- Review scaling, units, weights, outliers, and residual patterns.
- Distinguish prediction, explanation, calibration, and decision support.
