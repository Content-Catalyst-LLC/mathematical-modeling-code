# Ethics of Discrete Modeling

Discrete models can influence decisions about timing, thresholds, resources, health, infrastructure, finance, policy, and risk.

## Risks

- harms between steps are hidden;
- thresholds delay action until after burden accumulates;
- aggregated trajectories hide unequal impacts;
- boundary clipping hides shortage or failure;
- update order distorts policy timing;
- iterated scenarios appear inevitable.

## Responsible practice

- document step meaning;
- report boundary events;
- test threshold sensitivity;
- preserve trajectories;
- disaggregate where impacts differ;
- communicate projections as conditional scenarios.
