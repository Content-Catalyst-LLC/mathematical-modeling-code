# Residual Diagnostics Guide

Residuals are model-data differences after fitting.

## Review questions

- Are residuals centered around zero?
- Are residuals patterned over time, space, or fitted value?
- Do residuals grow as predictions increase?
- Are there outliers or regime-specific errors?
- Does the residual pattern imply missing structure?
- Are residuals smaller in calibration data than validation data?

## Principle

A low fitting error is not enough. Residual structure can reveal systematic model failure.
