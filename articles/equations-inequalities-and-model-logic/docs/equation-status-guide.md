# Equation Status Guide

## Definition

Introduces a derived quantity.

## Identity

Must hold by mathematics, accounting, or conservation.

## Balance equation

Tracks accumulation, inflow, outflow, and losses.

## Dynamic update

Moves a state from one time step to the next.

## Fitted relationship

Approximates observed data or statistical association.

## Measurement equation

Connects latent state to observations.

## Equilibrium equation

Defines a stationary or balanced condition.

## Review questions

- What kind of equation is this?
- Are both sides dimensionally consistent?
- Is the equation exact, approximate, fitted, assumed, or calibrated?
- Is it being interpreted beyond its status?
