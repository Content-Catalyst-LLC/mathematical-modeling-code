# Ethics of Equations, Inequalities, and Model Logic

Formal model logic can hide ethical choices.

## Risks

- objective equations optimize what is measurable rather than what matters;
- budget inequalities make scarcity appear neutral;
- eligibility rules exclude people or cases;
- safety thresholds normalize acceptable harm;
- penalty relaxation turns commitments into trade-offs;
- proxy equations equate measurement with deeper value.

## Responsible practice

- expose objectives and weights;
- distinguish physical constraints from policy constraints;
- document eligibility logic;
- state when constraints are binding and nonnegotiable;
- audit domain and transformation effects;
- distinguish model output from decision authority.
