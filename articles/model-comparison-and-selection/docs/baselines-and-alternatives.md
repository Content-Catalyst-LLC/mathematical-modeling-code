# Baselines and Alternatives

A model should rarely be selected without comparison to plausible alternatives.

## Baselines

- naive baseline;
- simple trend;
- mean or persistence model;
- established domain model;
- simpler version of candidate model.

## Candidate alternatives

- statistical models;
- mechanistic models;
- stochastic models;
- simulation models;
- regularized models;
- ensembles or scenario sets.

## Principle

A complex model should earn its complexity by improving evidence, explanation, robustness, or decision usefulness.
