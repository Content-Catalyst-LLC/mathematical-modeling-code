# Model Governance Guide

## Article

**Model Governance and Accountability**

## Central claim

A mathematical model used in decisions must be documented, reviewed, validated, monitored, revised, and retired through an accountable lifecycle process.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| model_governance_register | Tracks purpose, risk tier, validation status, use-limit status, monitoring status, model owner, and decision owner |
| model_governance_risk_review | Scores model risk from error, uncertainty, consequence, scope misuse, and accountability gaps |
| model_governance_card | Summarizes review status, escalation needs, use limits, and accountability |
| lifecycle_checklist | Defines design, validation, deployment, monitoring, revision, and retirement artifacts |
