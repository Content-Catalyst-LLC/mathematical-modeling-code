# Boundary Ethics

Boundary choices determine what becomes visible and what disappears.

## Ethical review questions

- Are affected groups excluded from the unit of analysis?
- Are harms outside the model boundary?
- Are downstream effects omitted?
- Are long-term consequences excluded?
- Are administrative boundaries hiding ecological or social systems?
- Are existing institutional limits treated as natural constraints?
- Does the model output travel beyond the validation domain?

## Responsible practice

- publish boundary registers;
- report subgroup or local outputs where relevant;
- label exploratory outputs;
- define prohibited uses;
- separate model evidence from decision authority.
