# Boundary Validation Plan

## Validation question

Is the model adequate for its intended purpose, within its stated boundary, at its stated scale, and under its stated scope?

## Boundary tests

- add omitted drivers;
- add feedback loops;
- expand time horizon;
- compare aggregate and subgroup outputs;
- compare spatial resolutions;
- test external shock scenarios;
- compare policy and no-policy boundary versions.

## Evidence requirements

- empirical observations;
- domain review;
- data coverage assessment;
- time horizon adequacy;
- scope statement;
- prohibited-use statement;
- boundary sensitivity outputs.
