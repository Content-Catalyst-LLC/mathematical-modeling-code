# Validation Limits Under Structural Uncertainty

Validation reduces uncertainty within a domain of use. It does not prove that a model form is universally correct.

## Validation questions

- Does validation cover the intended use context?
- Does validation include threshold or stress conditions?
- Do residuals suggest missing structure?
- Does the model transfer across scale, time, group, or scenario?
- Do alternative model forms produce similar conclusions?

## Principle

A model can pass historical validation and still fail when the system moves into a different regime.
