# Structural Uncertainty Guide

## Article

**Structural Uncertainty and Model Form Error**

## Central claim

Some uncertainty lives in the form of the model. A model can be well calibrated and still structurally wrong if its equations, boundaries, mechanisms, scale, aggregation, or model family are inappropriate for the intended purpose.

## Required records

| Record | Purpose |
|---|---|
| model_form_register | Documents plausible model structures |
| structural_uncertainty_register | Tracks model family, functional form, boundary, aggregation, scale, and regime uncertainty |
| model_form_comparison | Compares outputs across structures |
| structural_summary | Reports structural spread and threshold disagreement |
| use_limit_statement | Documents where single-model authority is not warranted |
