# Ecology and Sustainability Modeling Guide

## Article

**Mathematical Modeling in Ecology and Sustainability**

## Central claim

Ecological and sustainability models are dynamic representations that connect stocks, flows, feedbacks, thresholds, climate stress, human pressures, uncertainty, and stewardship decisions.

## Required artifacts

| Artifact | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ecology_model_register | Tracks domain, role, model family, sustainability question, and status |
| sustainability_scenario_review | Compares resource-stock scenarios and threshold risk |
| baseline_resource_trajectory | Provides a reproducible stock and resilience-margin trajectory |
| sustainability_review_card | Summarizes ecological evidence, diagnostics, thresholds, and use limits |
| governance_record | Documents adaptive management, monitoring, and stewardship review |
