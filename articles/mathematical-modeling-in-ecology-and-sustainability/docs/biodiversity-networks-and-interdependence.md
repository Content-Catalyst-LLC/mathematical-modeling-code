# Biodiversity, Networks, and Interdependence

Biodiversity models should include more than species counts when possible.

## Review questions

- Which species, habitats, or interactions are represented?
- Which keystone roles or dependencies matter?
- What interaction data are missing?
- Does fragmentation affect connectivity?
- Could the loss of one node trigger indirect effects?

## Principle

Ecological systems are relational. Missing interactions can change model conclusions.
