# Resilience, Thresholds, and Adaptive Management

Sustainability modeling should track resilience margins and warning conditions.

## Review targets

- minimum ecological stock or condition;
- distance to threshold;
- recovery rate;
- climate stress;
- extraction or disturbance;
- monitoring trigger;
- adaptive policy response.

## Principle

Thresholds are often uncertain. Responsible management preserves buffer rather than waiting for exact proof of collapse.
