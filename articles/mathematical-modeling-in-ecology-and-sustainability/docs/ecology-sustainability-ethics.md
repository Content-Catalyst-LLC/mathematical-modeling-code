# Ecology and Sustainability Modeling Ethics

Ecological models can influence conservation, development, climate adaptation, resource access, environmental risk, Indigenous rights, and intergenerational justice.

## Ethical risks

- false precision;
- boundary injustice;
- threshold overconfidence;
- greenwashing;
- exclusion of local or Indigenous knowledge;
- short-term benefits hiding long-term ecological harm.

## Responsible practice

- document assumptions and limits;
- communicate uncertainty;
- review distributional consequences;
- include field evidence and local knowledge where appropriate;
- preserve adaptive monitoring;
- assign governance responsibility.
