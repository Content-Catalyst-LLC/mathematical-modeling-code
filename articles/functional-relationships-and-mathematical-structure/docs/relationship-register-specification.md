# Relationship Register Specification

## Article

**Functional Relationships and Mathematical Structure**

## Central claim

Functional relationships are not just formulas. They are claims about dependence, interaction, change, uncertainty, constraints, and decision logic.

## Required fields

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| key | Stable identifier |
| relationship_type | linear, nonlinear, dynamic, stochastic, feedback, threshold, networked, constraint, or optimization |
| expression | Formal expression or algorithmic rule |
| interpretation | Plain-language meaning |
| structural_assumption | Assumption embedded in the relationship |
| review_question | Question for structural review |
| status | active, review, revise, or archive |

## Review logic

High-priority relationships include those that:

- control conclusions;
- assume linearity;
- introduce stochasticity;
- enforce constraints;
- represent feedback;
- define thresholds or regimes;
- embed objective functions;
- connect model output to decisions.
