# Ethics of Mathematical Structure

Mathematical structure shapes what the model can see and what users may believe.

## Ethical review questions

- Does a linear structure hide thresholds or disproportionate effects?
- Does an additive structure hide interaction among risks or harms?
- Does a deterministic structure imply false certainty?
- Does a network structure reveal or obscure relational vulnerability?
- Does an objective function hide value judgments?
- Does a feedback omission hide adaptation or policy resistance?
- Does a black-box relationship prevent accountability?

## Responsible practice

- state structural assumptions clearly;
- compare plausible alternatives;
- test boundary behavior;
- communicate uncertainty;
- expose objectives and constraints;
- distinguish model evidence from decision authority.
