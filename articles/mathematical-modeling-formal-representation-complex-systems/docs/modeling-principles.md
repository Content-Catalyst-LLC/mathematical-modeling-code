# Mathematical Modeling Principles

## 1. Begin with purpose
A model should be designed for a specific use: explanation, prediction, control, simulation, decision support, or exploration.

## 2. Make assumptions visible
Assumptions are part of model architecture.

## 3. Simplify carefully
A good model is simplified enough to reason with and faithful enough to remain meaningful.

## 4. Separate variables and parameters
Variables change within the model. Parameters shape the model's behavior.

## 5. Test sensitivity
Determine whether conclusions depend strongly on uncertain assumptions or parameters.

## 6. Validate in context
Validation depends on purpose, evidence, domain, and required reliability.

## 7. Preserve uncertainty
Uncertainty should be represented, not hidden.

## 8. Document reproducibly
Data, code, parameters, outputs, and interpretation notes should be auditable.
