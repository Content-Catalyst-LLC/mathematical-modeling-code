# Objective Function Review Guide

## Core checks

- What does the model optimize?
- What does the objective omit?
- Are weights, penalties, or priorities documented?
- Is the objective aligned with decision purpose?
- Does the objective hide distributional impacts?
- Is cost, risk, equity, quality, or resilience treated as an objective or constraint?
- Does the model preserve near-optimal alternatives?

## Common failures

- optimizing what is measurable rather than what matters;
- treating weights as technical rather than value-laden;
- cost-only optimization;
- expected-value optimization without tail review;
- solver output presented as decision authority.
