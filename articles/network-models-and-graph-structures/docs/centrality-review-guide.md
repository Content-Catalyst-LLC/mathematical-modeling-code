# Centrality Review Guide

Centrality is not a synonym for importance. It is a mathematical diagnostic whose meaning depends on graph structure and modeling purpose.

Review questions:

- Which centrality measure is being used?
- What kind of importance does it claim to measure?
- Does the process follow shortest paths, direct ties, or weighted flow?
- Are rankings robust to missing edges or uncertain weights?
- Are high-centrality nodes actionable, vulnerable, powerful, exposed, or merely visible?
