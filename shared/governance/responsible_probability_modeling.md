# Responsible Probability Modeling

Probability models should represent uncertainty transparently and avoid false precision.

## Guardrails

- Document distributional assumptions.
- State whether probabilities are empirical, simulated, assumed, or elicited.
- Test sensitivity to priors, distributions, and dependence assumptions.
- Report tail behavior when risk matters.
- Avoid treating expected value as a complete risk summary.
- Communicate uncertainty without overstating certainty.
